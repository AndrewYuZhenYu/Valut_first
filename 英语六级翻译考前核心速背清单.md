## 二、20 个保底句式骨架（考试时直接换内容）

1. **[事物] dates back to [时期].**  
   _例如：Chinese calligraphy dates back thousands of years._
2. **[事物], which has a history of [时间], is known for [特征].**  
   _例如：The bridge, which has a history of more than 1,000 years, is known for its ingenious design._
3. **Located in [地点], [主体] is one of [范围] ...**  
   _例如：Located in Shanghai, the port is one of the major transport hubs in the region._
4. **[主体] is equipped with [设施] and can [能力].**  
   _例如：The hospital is equipped with modern equipment and can provide remote consultations._
5. **[主体] serves as an important [身份] connecting [A] with [B].**  
   _例如：The bridge serves as an important transport link connecting the two cities._
6. **In recent years, [领域] has made remarkable progress in [方面].**  
   _例如：In recent years, China has made remarkable progress in space technology._
7. **With the development of [技术], [现象] has become increasingly common.**  
   _例如：With the development of digital technology, online consultations have become increasingly common._
8. **[主体] has undergone great changes over the past [时间].**  
   _例如：Rural communities have undergone great changes over the past decade._
9. **[主体] is playing an increasingly important role in [领域].**  
   _例如：Renewable energy is playing an increasingly important role in electricity generation._
10. **[主体] has independently developed [设备/系统] to [目的].**  
   _例如：Researchers have independently developed a system to improve crop monitoring._
11. **[技术] is widely used in [领域A], [领域B] and [领域C].**  
   _例如：Satellite navigation is widely used in transport, agriculture and disaster response._
12. **By using [技术], [主体] can [结果].**  
   _例如：By using drones, farmers can monitor large areas of farmland more efficiently._
13. **The application of [技术] has made it possible to [行为].**  
   _例如：The application of telemedicine has made it possible to consult doctors remotely._
14. **The government has introduced a series of measures to [目标].**  
   _例如：The government has introduced a series of measures to improve healthcare services._
15. **To [目标], [主体] has stepped up efforts to [行动].**  
   _例如：To protect the environment, cities have stepped up efforts to reduce emissions._
16. **[主体] has made a significant contribution to [领域].**  
   _例如：Hybrid rice has made a significant contribution to global food security._
17. **[措施] not only [结果A] but also [结果B].**  
   _例如：The project not only improves connectivity but also creates job opportunities._
18. **[行动] helps to [结果A] while [结果B].**  
   _例如：Smart irrigation helps to save water while improving crop yields._
19. **Thanks to [有利因素], [结果].**  
   _例如：Thanks to improved infrastructure, travel has become much easier._
20. **Compared with [过去/对象], [主体] is now [变化].**  
   _例如：Compared with the past, public transport is now much more convenient._

## 三、考前最易错 15 项

1. 随着……发展：`as ... develops` / `with the development of ...`。
2. 广泛应用：`be widely used/applied in ...`。
3. 发挥重要作用：`play an important role in ...`。
4. 作贡献：`make a contribution to ...`。
5. 取得进展：`make progress in ...`。
6. 改善生活水平：`improve living standards`。
7. 提高效率：`improve efficiency`。
8. 促进发展：`promote development`。
9. 科研不可数：`conduct research`。
10. `infrastructure`、`equipment`、`information` 通常不可数。
11. `increase by 20%`（增加了 20%）与 `increase to 20%`（增至 20%）不同。
12. `in recent years` 通常搭配现在完成时描述持续变化。
13. `not only ... but also ...` 两侧结构保持平行。
14. `despite` 后接名词或 `-ing`，`although` 后接完整从句。
15. 不确定长难句就拆句，**信息完整与语法正确先于高级词汇**。

## 四、最后 20 分钟检查顺序

**漏译信息 → 专名/数字 → 主谓一致 → 时态 → 冠词/单复数 → 固定搭配 → 词序 → 拼写。**
