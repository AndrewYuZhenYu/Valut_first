## 1. Definition of Culture（文化的定义）

### 1.1 What Is Culture?（什么是文化？）

**Culture is the sum total of the ways of life, beliefs, customs, values, and behaviors shared by a group of people.**

文化是一个群体所共有的生活方式、信仰、习俗、价值观和行为方式的总和。

Culture influences how people think, behave, communicate, and understand the world.

文化影响人们如何思考、行动、交流以及理解世界。

### 1.2 Major Definitions of Culture（文化的主要定义）

#### Edward B. Tylor (1871)

Culture is a complex whole that includes knowledge, beliefs, art, morals, laws, customs, and other abilities and habits acquired by individuals as members of society.

文化是一个复杂的整体，包括知识、信仰、艺术、道德、法律、习俗，以及个人作为社会成员所习得的其他能力和习惯。

#### Kroeber and Kluckhohn (1952)

Culture consists of explicit and implicit patterns of behavior acquired and transmitted through symbols. Traditional ideas and values form its essential core.

文化由通过符号习得和传递的显性及隐性行为模式构成，其中传统思想和价值观是文化的核心。

#### Porter and Samovar

Culture includes accumulated knowledge, experiences, beliefs, values, attitudes, meanings, social hierarchies, religion, concepts of time, social roles, and material possessions.

文化包括世代积累的知识、经验、信仰、价值观、态度、意义、社会等级、宗教、时间观念、社会角色和物质财富等。

#### Yu Qiuyu（余秋雨）

Culture refers to spiritual values and ways of life that have become habitual.

文化是一种成为习惯的精神价值和生活方式。

### 1.3 Key Understanding（核心理解）

Culture is not limited to visible things such as buildings, clothing, and food. It also includes invisible elements such as beliefs, values, attitudes, and ways of thinking.

文化不仅包括建筑、服饰、食物等可见的事物，还包括信仰、价值观、态度和思维方式等不可见的要素。

## 2. Levels of Culture（文化的三个层次）

Culture can be divided into three major levels.

文化可以划分为三个主要层次。

| Level | Main Components | 中文翻译 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Material Culture** | Buildings, clothing, food, tools, and other physical objects | **物质文化**：建筑、服饰、食品、工具等有形事物 |
| **Institutional Culture / Norms** | Laws, institutions, social rules, customs, and behavioral standards | **制度与习俗文化**：法律、制度、社会规范、习俗和行为准则 |
| **Spiritual Culture / Ideas** | Values, beliefs, religion, morality, ways of thinking, and aesthetic preferences | **精神文化**：价值观、信仰、宗教、道德、思维方式和审美观念 |

### Surface Culture and Deep Culture（表层文化与深层文化）

**Surface Culture** refers to the visible and observable aspects of culture, such as appearance, clothing, food, and behavior.

表层文化是指可以直接观察到的文化要素，例如外貌、服饰、食物和行为。

**Deep Culture** refers to less visible elements, including values, assumptions, beliefs, attitudes, and worldviews.

深层文化是指不容易直接观察到的文化要素，包括价值观、假设、信仰、态度和世界观。

This distinction is closely connected to the iceberg metaphor of culture.

这种区分与文化冰山模型密切相关。

## 3. Five Metaphors of Culture（文化的五种隐喻）

The slides introduce five metaphors proposed by Linell Davis to explain the nature and influence of culture.

课件介绍了 Linell Davis 提出的五种文化隐喻，用来解释文化的本质及其影响。

### 3.1 Culture Is Like an Iceberg（文化就像冰山）

Only a small part of culture is visible, while most cultural elements remain hidden beneath the surface.

只有一小部分文化是可见的，而大部分文化要素隐藏在表面之下。

The slides use approximately 10% for visible culture and 90% for invisible culture as an illustration.

课件用约 10% 的可见部分和 90% 的不可见部分来说明这一模型（属于示意比例）。

| Visible Culture (10%) | Invisible Culture (90%) |
| --- | --- |
| Customs（习俗） | Values（价值观） |
| Clothing（服饰） | Beliefs（信仰） |
| Food（食物） | Assumptions（潜在假设） |
| Language（语言） | Attitudes（态度） |
| Greetings（问候方式） | Perceptions（认知方式） |
| Music and Art（音乐与艺术） | Worldviews（世界观） |
| Rituals（仪式） | Ways of Thinking（思维方式） |

> **Key Point:** To understand another culture, we must look beyond visible behavior and explore the underlying beliefs and values.
>
> **核心要点：** 要理解另一种文化，不能只观察外在行为，还需要了解其背后的信仰和价值观。

### 3.2 Culture Is Our Software（文化是人的软件）

The human body can be compared to computer hardware, while culture functions like software that influences how people think and behave.

人的身体可以比作计算机硬件，而文化则像软件一样影响人们的思维和行为。

Culture provides patterns and rules that guide human behavior.

文化提供指导人类行为的模式和规则。

### 3.3 Culture Is Like the Water a Fish Swims In（文化就像鱼生活的水）

Just as a fish may not notice the water surrounding it, people are often unaware of the culture that shapes their everyday lives.

正如鱼可能意识不到周围的水，人们也常常意识不到塑造其日常生活的文化环境。

People often become more aware of their own culture when they encounter a different culture.

当人们接触另一种文化时，往往会更加意识到自身文化的存在。

### 3.4 Culture Is the Story We Tell Ourselves About Ourselves（文化是我们讲述自身的故事）

People use stories to explain who they are, where they come from, and what they value.

人们通过故事来解释自己是谁、来自哪里，以及重视什么。

Cultural stories may change as societies adapt to new circumstances.

随着社会适应新的环境，文化故事也可能发生变化。

### 3.5 Culture Is the Grammar of Our Behavior（文化是行为的语法）

Just as grammar provides rules for language, culture provides rules for appropriate social behavior.

正如语法为语言提供规则，文化也为恰当的社会行为提供规则。

People often learn and apply cultural rules unconsciously.

人们往往在无意识中学习并运用文化规则。

**Example:** Greeting customs differ across cultures. People may shake hands, bow, or use other forms of greeting depending on their cultural background.

**例子：** 不同文化的问候习惯存在差异。人们可能根据自身文化背景采取握手、鞠躬或其他问候方式。

## 4. Seven Characteristics of Culture（文化的七大特征）

This is one of the central theoretical sections of the chapter.

这是本章最重要的理论部分之一。

### 4.1 Culture Is Learned（文化具有习得性）

Culture is not biologically inherited. People learn their culture through social experiences, both consciously and unconsciously.

文化不是通过生物遗传直接获得的，而是人们通过社会经历有意识或无意识地学习得到的。

**Enculturation（文化习得）** is the process through which individuals learn and assimilate the practices, traditions, and values of their own culture.

文化习得是个人学习并吸收自身文化的行为习惯、传统和价值观的过程。

The slides identify several ways of learning culture:

课件列出了以下文化学习途径：

| Method | 中文翻译 |
| --- | --- |
| Proverbs | 谚语 |
| Folktales, legends, and myths | 民间故事、传说和神话 |
| Art | 艺术 |
| Mass media | 大众媒体 |

For example, proverbs communicate cultural values and lessons across generations.

例如，谚语能够将文化价值观和经验教训传递给后代。

### 4.2 Most of Culture Is Unconscious（文化的大部分影响是无意识的）

People frequently follow cultural rules without realizing that these rules exist.

人们经常在没有意识到文化规则存在的情况下遵循这些规则。

Many behaviors become so familiar that people consider them natural or universal.

许多行为因为过于熟悉而被人们视为自然的，甚至认为它们具有普遍性。

This characteristic is closely related to the iceberg metaphor.

这一特征与文化冰山模型密切相关。

### 4.3 Culture Is Shared（文化具有共享性）

Members of a cultural group share important values, ideas, beliefs, and ways of understanding the world.

同一文化群体的成员共享重要的价值观、思想、信仰和理解世界的方式。

Shared culture helps create a sense of belonging and group identity.

文化的共享性有助于形成归属感和群体认同。

### 4.4 Culture Is Transmitted from Generation to Generation（文化具有代际传递性）

Culture is passed from one generation to the next through teaching, imitation, communication, and social interaction.

文化通过教育、模仿、交流和社会互动从一代传递到下一代。

This transmission helps preserve cultural traditions and values.

这种传递有助于保存文化传统和价值观。

### 4.5 Culture Is Based on Symbols（文化以符号为基础）

Symbols represent meanings shared by members of a culture.

符号代表文化群体成员共同理解的意义。

The slides identify three major forms of cultural symbols:

课件列出了三种主要的文化符号形式：

- **Verbal codes:** books, writing, and speech.  
  **语言符号：** 书籍、文字和言语。
- **Non-verbal actions:** gestures and body movements.  
  **非语言行为：** 手势和身体动作。
- **Material objects:** pictures, flags, and clothing.  
  **物质符号：** 图片、旗帜和服装。

**Language is one of the most important cultural symbol systems.**

语言是最重要的文化符号系统之一。

### 4.6 Culture Is Dynamic / Subject to Change（文化具有动态性）

Culture changes over time as societies develop and interact with other cultures.

随着社会的发展以及不同文化之间的交流，文化会随时间发生变化。

Changes may occur in clothing, food, music, transportation, entertainment, and lifestyles.

文化变化可能体现在服饰、食物、音乐、交通、娱乐和生活方式等方面。

However, some core cultural values may remain relatively stable for long periods.

但是，一些核心文化价值观可能在很长一段时间内保持相对稳定。

**Acculturation（文化适应）** is a concept introduced in the slides in connection with cultural change.

文化适应是课件在讨论文化变化时引入的一个概念。

### 4.7 Culture Is an Integrated System（文化是一个相互联系的整体）

Different elements of culture are interconnected and influence one another.

文化的不同要素相互联系，并且彼此影响。

The slides use Confucianism as an example of how cultural beliefs influence Chinese attitudes toward the elderly, social harmony, cooperation, and seniority.

课件以儒家思想为例，说明文化观念如何影响中国人对老年人、社会和谐、合作以及长幼秩序的态度。

## 5. Dominant Culture and Co-cultures（主导文化与共存文化）

### 5.1 Dominant Culture（主导文化）

**Dominant culture**, also called **mainstream culture**, refers to the cultural beliefs, values, behaviors, and social norms widely shared and accepted by members of a society.

主导文化，也称主流文化，是指社会成员广泛共享和接受的文化信仰、价值观、行为方式和社会规范。

The slides use Han culture in China as an example of a dominant culture.

课件以中国的汉文化作为主导文化的例子。

### 5.2 Subculture（亚文化）

A **subculture** is a cultural group that exists within a larger culture but has its own distinctive beliefs, practices, or lifestyles.

亚文化是存在于较大文化体系内部，但具有自身独特信仰、习俗或生活方式的群体文化。

Subcultures may develop around social class, ethnicity, geographic region, profession, age, or other group identities.

亚文化可能围绕社会阶层、民族、地区、职业、年龄或其他群体身份形成。

**Examples from the slides:**

课件中的例子包括：

- Northern and Southern Chinese cultures（中国南北方文化）
- Tibetan culture（藏族文化）
- Youth culture（青年文化）
- Teacher and student cultures（教师文化与学生文化）
- Regional cultures（地域文化）

### 5.3 Co-culture（共存文化）

The term **co-culture** emphasizes that different cultural groups can coexist within the same society and should not automatically be considered inferior to the dominant culture.

“共存文化”这一术语强调，不同文化群体能够在同一社会中共存，不应仅因为它们不是主导文化就被认为地位较低。

The slides prefer this term in some contexts because it suggests greater equality between cultural groups.

课件指出，在某些情况下更倾向使用这一术语，因为它更能体现不同文化群体之间的平等关系。

## 6. Cultural Awareness（文化意识）

### 6.1 Definition of Cultural Awareness（文化意识的定义）

**Cultural awareness** is the ability to recognize and understand cultural values, beliefs, and customs that may differ from our own.

文化意识是指识别和理解不同于自身文化的价值观、信仰和习俗的能力。

What is considered normal or acceptable in one culture may seem unusual or unacceptable in another.

一种文化中被认为正常或可以接受的行为，在另一种文化中可能显得不寻常，甚至不可接受。

To communicate effectively across cultures, people should recognize cultural differences and respect the beliefs and customs of others.

为了实现有效的跨文化交际，人们应当认识文化差异，并尊重他人的信仰与习俗。

### 6.2 Time Orientation（时间取向）

The slides discuss two different cultural orientations toward time.

课件讨论了两种不同的文化时间取向。

| Orientation | English Explanation | 中文翻译 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Future-oriented Culture** | Emphasizes the future, change, taking chances, and youth. | **未来取向文化**：重视未来、变化、冒险和年轻人。 |
| **Past-oriented Culture** | Emphasizes history, established religions, traditions, and respect for the past. | **过去取向文化**：重视历史、传统宗教、传统习俗和对过去的尊重。 |

These orientations help explain why people from different cultural backgrounds may have different attitudes toward age, tradition, and social relationships.

这些取向有助于解释为什么不同文化背景的人在年龄、传统和社会关系方面可能具有不同的态度。

## 7. Intercultural Communication Cases（跨文化交际案例分析）

The slides use several cases to demonstrate how differences in language, customs, values, and social expectations can lead to misunderstandings.

课件通过多个案例说明，语言、习俗、价值观和社会期望方面的差异如何导致误解。

### Case 1 — Girl-ness（称呼女大学生为 Girls）

**Situation:** A Chinese visiting professor refers to female university students as *girls*. An American female student objects to this expression.

**情境：** 一位中国访问教授将女大学生称为 *girls*，一位美国女大学生对这一表达提出异议。

**Reason:** In this case, the American student considers *women* more appropriate for adult female students. She regards *girls* as disrespectful.

**原因：** 在这个案例中，美国女大学生认为成年女性大学生更适合被称为 *women*，而 *girls* 可能被理解为不尊重。

**Cultural lesson:** Words that seem neutral in one culture may carry different meanings in another.

**文化启示：** 在一种文化中看似中性的词语，在另一种文化中可能具有不同的含义。

### Case 2 — An Unwanted Food Service（不受欢迎的食物服务）

**Situation:** A Chinese interpreter repeatedly offers food to foreign participants even after they refuse.

**情境：** 一位中国翻译在外国参会人员已经拒绝之后，仍然反复劝他们吃东西。

**Reason:** The interpreter intends to show hospitality, but the foreign participants may interpret the repeated offers as unnecessary pressure.

**原因：** 翻译希望表达热情好客，但外国参会人员可能将反复劝食理解为不必要的压力。

**Cultural lesson:** Expressions of politeness and hospitality may differ across cultures.

**文化启示：** 不同文化表达礼貌和热情好客的方式可能不同。

### Case 3 — White Elephant（白象电池的品牌名称）

**Situation:** A Chinese battery brand uses the English name *White Elephant*.

**情境：** 一个中国电池品牌使用 *White Elephant* 作为英文名称。

**Reason:** In English, *a white elephant* can refer to something expensive to maintain but of little practical value.

**原因：** 英语中的 *a white elephant* 可以指维护成本高、但实际价值很小的东西。

**Cultural lesson:** The same symbol or expression may have different cultural associations.

**文化启示：** 同一个符号或表达在不同文化中可能产生不同的联想。

### Case 4 — Why Don't You Eat the Pizza?（为什么不吃披萨？）

**Situation:** Kenneth, an American student, gives pizza to Vernon, a Malaysian student, using his left hand. Vernon refuses to eat it.

**情境：** 美国学生 Kenneth 用左手将披萨递给马来西亚学生 Vernon，但 Vernon 拒绝食用。

**Reason:** The slides explain that in the religious and cultural context involved, the left hand is associated with bodily cleaning and is considered inappropriate for handling food.

**原因：** 课件解释，在该案例涉及的宗教与文化习俗中，左手与身体清洁活动有关，因此被认为不适合传递食物。

**Cultural lesson:** Religious beliefs and social customs can influence everyday behavior and communication.

**文化启示：** 宗教信仰和社会习俗能够影响日常行为和人际交际。

### Case 5 — Introducing Miss Brown（介绍美国女教师）

**Situation:** A Chinese school principal introduces an American teacher by describing her as a “very pretty girl” and a “very good teacher.”

**情境：** 一位中国学校校长在介绍美国女教师时，称她为一位“非常漂亮的女孩”和“非常好的老师”。

**Reason:** The introduction emphasizes physical appearance and subjective judgments rather than professional identity and qualifications.

**原因：** 这一介绍强调外貌和主观评价，而不是教师的职业身份和专业资格。

**Cultural lesson:** In a formal professional introduction, objective information about qualifications and experience may be more appropriate.

**文化启示：** 在正式职业场合介绍他人时，客观介绍其专业资格和工作经历通常更加恰当。

### Case 6 — An American Is Sick（关心生病的美国同事）

**Situation:** A Chinese colleague expresses concern for a sick American by giving repeated advice about medicine, clothing, water, and rest.

**情境：** 一位中国同事通过反复建议生病的美国同事吃药、添衣、喝水和休息来表达关心。

**Reason:** The American colleague interprets these repeated suggestions as excessive interference with personal independence.

**原因：** 美国同事将这些反复提出的建议理解为对个人独立性的过度干涉。

**Cultural lesson:** Caring for others does not always require giving advice. Sometimes expressing sympathy and concern is more appropriate.

**文化启示：** 表达关心并不一定需要提出大量建议，有时表达同情与慰问更加合适。

## 9. Review Questions（复习思考题）

### Question 1. What is culture?（什么是文化？）

**Answer:** Culture includes the shared ways of life, beliefs, customs, values, and behaviors of a group of people.

**参考答案：** 文化包括一个群体共享的生活方式、信仰、习俗、价值观和行为方式。

### Question 2. What are the three levels of culture?（文化的三个层次是什么？）

**Answer:** Material culture, institutional or customary culture, and spiritual culture.

**参考答案：** 物质文化、制度与习俗文化、精神文化。

### Question 3. What are the five metaphors of culture?（文化的五种隐喻是什么？）

**Answer:** Culture is like an iceberg; our software; the water a fish swims in; the story we tell ourselves about ourselves; and the grammar of our behavior.

**参考答案：** 文化像冰山、文化是软件、文化像鱼生活的水、文化是我们讲述自身的故事、文化是行为的语法。

### Question 4. What are the seven characteristics of culture?（文化的七大特征是什么？）

**Answer:** Culture is learned, largely unconscious, shared, transmitted across generations, based on symbols, dynamic, and integrated.

**参考答案：** 文化具有习得性、无意识性、共享性、代际传递性、符号性、动态性和整体性。

### Question 5. What is the difference between dominant culture and co-culture?（主导文化与共存文化有什么区别？）

**Answer:** Dominant culture refers to widely accepted mainstream cultural patterns, while co-culture emphasizes different cultural groups coexisting within a society.

**参考答案：** 主导文化指社会中广泛接受的主流文化模式；共存文化强调同一社会中不同文化群体的共同存在。

### Question 6. Why is cultural awareness important?（为什么文化意识很重要？）

**Answer:** It helps people recognize cultural differences, avoid misunderstandings, and communicate more respectfully.

**参考答案：** 它帮助人们认识文化差异、避免误解，并以更加尊重的方式进行交际。

## Overall Conclusion（总体总结）

Culture is learned, shared, transmitted, symbolic, dynamic, and deeply connected to human behavior. Understanding both visible and invisible cultural elements is essential for successful intercultural communication.

文化通过学习获得，在群体中共享并代际传递，依托符号表达，随着时间发展变化，并深刻影响人类行为。理解可见与不可见的文化要素，是成功进行跨文化交际的重要基础。
