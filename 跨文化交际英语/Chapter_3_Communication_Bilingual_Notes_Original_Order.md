# Chapter 3 — Communication（交际）

## 1. Cultural Awareness（文化意识）

### 1.1 Five Metaphors of Culture（文化的五种隐喻）

**1. Culture is like an iceberg.** Some is visible (history, literature, customs); most is invisible (feelings and attitudes).

**文化就像冰山。** 一部分文化是可见的，如历史、文学和习俗；大部分文化是不可见的，如情感和态度。

**2. Culture is our software.** Physical serves as the hardware, become human when programmed with the software of culture.

**文化是我们的软件。** 人的身体是硬件，经过文化软件的“编程”，人形成具有文化特征的行为和生活方式。

**3. Culture is like the water a fish swims in.** Notice everything except the water.

**文化就像鱼生活的水。** 鱼注意到周围的一切，却没有注意到水本身。

**4. Culture is the story we tell ourselves about ourselves.** People tell stories to tell who they are and stories also change to adapt to changing circumstances.

**文化是我们讲述自身的故事。** 人们通过故事说明自己的身份，这些故事也会随着环境变化而改变。

**5. Culture is the grammar of our behavior.** People learn their cultural grammar unconsciously and apply them automatically in order to behave appropriately in any society.

**文化是行为的语法。** 人们无意识地习得自己的文化语法，并自动运用这些规则，以便在任何社会中表现得恰当。

#### Surface Culture and Deep Culture（表层文化与深层文化）

| Visible — Surface Culture（可见的表层文化） | Less Visible — Deep Culture（不易察觉的深层文化） |
|---|---|
| Behaviors（行为） | Attitudes（态度） |
| Appearance（外貌与外在表现） | Assumptions（假设） |
| Symbols（符号） | Beliefs（信仰） |
| Traditions（传统） | Values（价值观） |
| Artefacts: buildings, clothes, art, etc.（人工制品：建筑、服饰、艺术品等） | — |

### 1.2 Definition of Cultural Awareness（文化意识的定义）

**Cultural awareness** is the ability to look outside of ourselves and be aware of the cultural values, and customs of the culture we are in.

**文化意识**是指能够跳出自身视角，意识到所处文化中的文化价值观和习俗的能力。

What may be normal and acceptable to us, may be unusual or unacceptable in another culture. When we are traveling, or around people from a different culture we need to be aware of their beliefs and customs, and respect them.

对我们而言正常且可以接受的事物，在另一种文化中可能显得不同寻常，甚至不可接受。当我们旅行或与不同文化背景的人交往时，应认识并尊重他们的信仰和习俗。

### 1.3 Four Levels of Cross-cultural Awareness（跨文化意识的四个层级）

**Robert G. Hanvey's four levels** distinguish cultural awareness according to the information people possess, the way they acquire it, and how they interpret cultural differences.

**Robert G. Hanvey 提出的四个层级**依据个人掌握的信息、获取知识的途径以及理解文化差异的方式来区分文化意识。

| Level（层级） | Information（掌握的信息） | Way of Knowing（认识途径） | Interpretation of Cultural Differences（对文化差异的理解） |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Awareness of superficial or very visible cultural traits; stereotypes.（认识表面或非常明显的文化特征；刻板印象。） | Tourism, textbooks, TV and films, popular opinion.（旅游、教科书、电视电影、大众观点。） | Unbelievable, exotic or bizarre, and possibly entertaining.（不可思议、充满异国情调或怪异，也可能令人觉得有趣。） |
| **2** | Awareness of significant and subtle cultural traits quite different from your own.（认识到与自身文化显著不同且细微的文化特征。） | Culture conflict situation.（文化冲突情境。） | Unbelievable in a frustrating way; it seems irrational.（令人沮丧地难以置信，似乎不合逻辑。） |
| **3** | Awareness of significant and subtle cultural traits quite different from your own.（认识到与自身文化显著不同且细微的文化特征。） | Study about the culture; formal study in school.（研究相关文化；在学校接受正式学习。） | Believable but only at a thinking level.（在认知层面能够理解，但尚未达到情感层面。） |
| **4** | Awareness of how a culture feels to someone who is a member of it.（能够感知某一文化的成员是如何体验其文化的。） | Getting into and living the culture.（进入该文化并在其中生活。） | Believable at an emotional level as lived experience.（通过亲身经历，在情感层面理解和接受。） |

## 2. Perspectives and Definitions of Communication（交际观念与定义）

### 2.1 Western Perspective of Communication（西方交际观）

In western cultures, communication is studied as the means of **transmitting ideas**. Western cultures emphasize the **instrumental function of communication**; that is, effectiveness is evaluated in terms of success in the manipulation of others to achieve one's personal goal.

在西方文化中，交际被视为**传递思想的手段**。西方文化强调交际的**工具性功能**，即以能否成功影响他人、实现个人目标来评价交际的有效性。

### 2.2 Eastern Perspective of Communication（东方交际观）

Definitions of communication from many Asian countries stress **harmony**, which is most notable in cultures with a Confucian tradition. Eastern cultures’ understanding would define communication as a process where all parties are searching to **develop and maintain a social relationship**.

许多亚洲国家对交际的定义强调**和谐**，这一特点在具有儒家传统的文化中尤为突出。交际被理解为各方努力**建立并维持社会关系**的过程。

| Western Perspective（西方交际观） | Eastern Perspective（东方交际观） |
|---|---|
| Transmitting ideas（传递思想） | Harmony（和谐） |
| Instrumental function（工具性功能） | Developing and maintaining social relationships（建立并维持社会关系） |
| Achieving personal goals（实现个人目标） | Maintaining relationships（维系关系） |

### 2.3 What Is Communication?（什么是交际？）

**Definition 1 — Information transmission（信息传递）**

> The activity or process of giving information to other people or to other living things, using signals such as speech, body movements, or radio signals, and so on.

交际是通过言语、身体动作、无线电信号等符号或信号，向他人或其他生物传递信息的活动或过程。

**Definition 2 — A dynamic symbolic process（动态的符号过程）**

> Communication is a dynamic process in which people attempt to share their internal states with other people through the use of symbols.

交际是一个动态过程，人们试图通过符号与他人分享自己的内在状态。

**Definition 3 — Source and receiver（发送者与接收者）**

**Oxford Companion to the English Language (McArthur, 1993):**

> Communication refers to the transmission of information (a massage) between source (sender) and receiver, by using signalling system.

交际是指发送者与接收者之间利用信号系统传递信息的过程。

**Definition 4 — Mutual understanding（相互理解）**

> Communication generally refers to the process in which participants create and share information with one another as they move toward reaching mutual understanding.

交际通常指参与者在努力实现相互理解的过程中，彼此创造并分享信息的过程。

> [!example] Communication Beyond Human Speech · 不限于人类言语的交际
> When you ask a dog to pick up a ball, signals are being used to communicate information.
>
> 当你要求狗捡起一个球时，你正在利用信号传递信息，因而参与了交际过程。

### 2.4 Harold Lasswell's Communication Formula（拉斯韦尔传播公式）

> **Who (says) what (in) which channel (to) whom (and with) what effect?**
>
> **谁 → 说什么 → 通过什么渠道 → 对谁说 → 产生什么效果？**

| Question | Component | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| Who? | Communicator / Sender | 传播者 / 发送者 |
| Says what? | Message | 讯息 / 信息 |
| In which channel? | Channel / Medium | 渠道 / 媒介 |
| To whom? | Receiver / Audience | 接收者 / 受众 |
| With what effect? | Effect | 效果 |

## 3. Components of Communication（交际的组成要素）

### 3.1 Basic Components（基本要素）

**1. Sender / Source（信息发送者 / 信息源）**

> A sender/source is the person who transmits a message.

发送者或信息源是传递信息的人。

**2. Message（信息）**

> A message is any signal that triggers the response of a receiver.

信息是能够引起接收者反应的任何信号。

**3. Encoding（编码）**

> Encoding refers to the activity during which the sender must choose certain words or nonverbal methods to send an intentional message.

编码是指发送者为了传递有意图的信息，选择特定词语或非语言方式的活动。

**4. Channel / Medium（渠道 / 媒介）**

> Channel/Medium is the method used to deliver a message.

渠道或媒介是传递信息所使用的方法。

**5. Receiver（信息接收者）**

> A receiver is any person who notices and gives some meaning to a message.

接收者是注意到信息并赋予其一定意义的人。

**6. Decoding（解码）**

> Decoding is the activity during which the receiver attaches meaning to the words or symbols he/she has received.

解码是指接收者为所接收到的词语或符号赋予意义的活动。

**7. Feedback（反馈）**

> The response of a receiver to a sender's message is called feedback.

接收者对发送者信息所作出的回应称为反馈。

**8. Noise（干扰）**

> Noise is a term used for factors that interfere with the exchange of messages, including external noise, physiological noise, psychological noise and semantic noise. Noise is inevitable.

干扰是指妨碍信息交换的因素，包括外部干扰、生理干扰、心理干扰和语义干扰。干扰是不可避免的。

### 3.2 Context and the Complete Process（情境与完整过程）

**Context（情境 / 背景 / 环境）**

> A setting or situation in which communication occurs.

交际发生的环境或情境。

**Communication process（交际过程）：**

```text
Sender → Encoding → Message → Channel → Receiver → Decoding → Response
  ↑                                                               │
  └──────────────────────── Feedback ─────────────────────────────┘

Context: The setting in which communication occurs
Noise: Factors that interfere with message exchange
```

**中文对应：**

```text
发送者 → 编码 → 信息 → 渠道 → 接收者 → 解码 → 反应
   ↑                                         │
   └──────────────── 反馈 ──────────────────┘

情境：交际发生的背景与环境
干扰：阻碍信息交换的因素
```

**Response（反应）** is the reaction of the receiver to the decoded message.

**反应**是接收者对已经解码的信息作出的反应。

**Feedback（反馈）** is a message about the effects of a previous message, sent back to the sender.

**反馈**是有关先前信息所产生效果的信息，并被传回原发送者。

#### Four Types of Noise（四种干扰）

The four types are **external noise, physiological noise, psychological noise, and semantic noise**. Noise is inevitable.

四种干扰分别是**外部干扰、生理干扰、心理干扰和语义干扰**。干扰是不可避免的。

### 3.3 Case Analysis: A Greeting on Campus（案例：校园问候）

**Conversation（对话）：**

> **Boy:** Hi, how are you doing?
>
> **Girl:** Not bad. How are you?
>
> **Boy:** Oh, fine. Busy as usual.
>
> **Girl:** Me too. …

**中文：**

> **男生：** 嗨，最近怎么样？
>
> **女生：** 还不错。你呢？
>
> **男生：** 哦，挺好的，像往常一样忙。
>
> **女生：** 我也是……

| Component | Application | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| Context | On campus, on the way to class | 在校园内，去上课的路上 |
| Sender | The boy | 男生 |
| Encoding | Converting the idea of greeting into a verbal message | 把问候的想法编码为语言信息 |
| Message | “How are you doing?” | “最近怎么样？” |
| Channel | Face to face | 面对面交谈 |
| Receiver | The girl | 女生 |
| Decoding | Understanding the message as a greeting | 将话语理解为问候 |
| Response | A positive response to the greeting | 对问候作出积极回应 |
| Feedback | “Not bad. How are you?” | “还不错。你呢？” |
| Noise | None | 无 |

## 4. Models and Basic Steps of Communication（交际模型与基本步骤）

### 4.1 Linear Model of Communication（线性传播模型）

**Linear Model of Communication** places the sender and receiver in a one-directional process involving encoding, a message/channel, decoding and noise.

**线性传播模型**将发送者和接收者置于单向交际过程中，涉及编码、信息与渠道、解码以及干扰。

```text
Sender → Encoding → Message / Channel → Decoding → Receiver
                        ↑
                      Noise
```

### 4.2 Interactive Model of Communication（互动传播模型）

**Interactive Model of Communication** includes feedback. **Feedback is essential to good communication.**

**互动传播模型**将反馈纳入交际过程。**反馈对于良好交际至关重要。**

```text
Sender → Message / Channel → Receiver
  ↑                             │
  └──── Feedback / Message ──────┘
```

### 4.3 Transactional Model of Communication（交易式 / 交互式传播模型）

**Transactional Model of Communication** emphasizes that **we continually influence each other through communication**. It includes **fields of experience** and **relationship dimensions**.

**交易式传播模型**强调**交际双方通过交流持续地相互影响**，并考虑**经验领域**和**关系维度**。

| Model | Main Focus | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| Linear Model | Sender → Receiver | 发送者向接收者单向传递信息 |
| Interactive Model | Feedback | 强调反馈 |
| Transactional Model | Continual mutual influence; fields of experience; relationship dimensions | 强调持续相互影响、经验领域及关系维度 |

### 4.4 Six Basic Steps of Interaction（互动的六个基本步骤）

**Step 1 — Idea（想法）**

> The communication process is sparked by the emotional impulse to transmit information (a thought / meaning) — data, or/and a personal emotion — to someone else.

交际过程始于向他人传递信息的冲动，信息可能是思想、意义、数据或个人情感。

**Step 2 — Encode（编码）**

> To package the information for transmission, we clarify it in our minds and “encode” it using language. We try to transmit what we mean.

为了传递信息，我们先在头脑中明确其含义，再运用语言进行“编码”，力图传达自己想表达的意思。

**Step 3 — Transmit Message（传递信息）**

> We transmit the information using body language and speech — words, gestures, intonations of voice. (We assume that all these elements make our meaning and intent clear to the receiver.)

我们通过身体语言和言语传递信息，包括词语、手势和语调。（我们认为这些要素能让接收者清楚地理解我们的意义与意图。）

**Step 4 — Receive（接收）**

> The transmission is received. The Receiver hears (all or some of) the spoken words and their intonation, and notes (all or some of) the gestures and the body language.

接收者收到信息，听到全部或部分话语及其语调，并注意到全部或部分手势与身体语言。

**Step 5 — Decode（解码）**

> The Receiver interprets or “decodes” the transmission. Drawing on knowledge, experience, and innate personality, the receiver interprets the message, weighs the importance and significance of the words, the gestures, body language, and vocal style. From this, the receiver determines what he/she believes is the “true” meaning and sentiment of the message.

接收者对信息进行解释或“解码”。他会结合知识、经验和自身个性理解信息，判断词语、手势、身体语言和语音风格的重要性与意义，由此确定自己认为的信息“真实”含义和情感。

**Step 6 — Feedback（反馈）**

> The receiver responds to the message. Whatever this involves — overt action, a change of opinion or emotion, increased understanding — any message of substance causes a reaction.

接收者对信息作出回应。无论这种回应是明显行动、观点或情感的改变，还是理解程度的提高，具有实际意义的信息都会引起某种反应。

> [!important] Six-Step Sequence · 六步顺序
> **Idea → Encode → Transmit Message → Receive → Decode → Feedback**
>
> **想法 → 编码 → 传递信息 → 接收 → 解码 → 反馈**

## 5. Review and Applied Communication（复习与交际应用）

### 5.1 Key Concept Checks（核心概念辨析）

**Question 1 — Encoding（编码）**

**“_________ occurs when the source creates a message through the selection of verbal or nonverbal symbols.”**

“当信息源选择语言或非语言符号形成信息时，发生的是_________。”

- A. noise（干扰）
- B. decoding（解码）
- C. encoding（编码）
- D. feedback（反馈）

**Answer: C. Encoding（编码）**

**Question 2 — Context（情境）**

**“Communication does not happen in isolation and it must happen in a setting or context. Therefore, communication is ________.”**

“交际不是孤立发生的，而必须发生在某种环境或情境中。因此交际具有________特征。”

- A. contextual（情境性的）
- B. irreversible（不可逆的）
- C. dynamic（动态的）
- D. symbolic（符号性的）

**Answer: A. Contextual（情境性的）**

**Question 3 — Metaphors of Culture（文化隐喻，多选）**

**“Which of the following metaphors or similes can be used to describe the nature of culture?”**

“以下哪些隐喻或明喻可以用来描述文化的本质？”

- A. Culture is our software.（文化是我们的软件。）
- B. Culture is the grammar of our behavior.（文化是我们行为的语法。）
- C. Culture is like the fish.（文化就像鱼。）
- D. Culture is like an onion.（文化像洋葱。）

**Answer: ABD**

**Question 4 — Cultural Noise（文化干扰）**

**“________ takes place when I speak a foreign language, my mother tongue affects the way I use it.”**

“当我说外语时，母语影响我使用外语的方式，这种现象属于________。”

**Answer: Cultural noise（文化干扰）**

### 5.2 Case Study: Showing Concern for a Sick Colleague（案例：关心生病的同事）

**Conversation（对话）：**

> **Chinese colleague:** You look pale. What's the matter?
>
> **American colleague:** I'm feeling sick. A cold, maybe.
>
> **Chinese colleague:** Go and see the doctor. Drink more water. Did you take any pills? Chinese medicine works wonderful. Would you like to try? Put on more clothes. Have a good rest.
>
> **American colleague:** You are not my mother, are you?

**中文：**

> **中国同事：** 你脸色很苍白，怎么了？
>
> **美国同事：** 我感觉不舒服，可能是感冒了。
>
> **中国同事：** 去看看医生吧。多喝水。吃药了吗？中药效果很好，要试一试吗？多穿点衣服，好好休息。
>
> **美国同事：** 你又不是我妈妈，对吧？

**Cultural difference:** The American colleague places considerable value on personal independence (**individual-oriented**). Receiving excessive care may be associated with weakness.

**文化差异：** 美国同事比较重视个人独立性（**个人取向**），受到过多照顾可能被视为弱者的表现。

**Proper responses（恰当的回应）：**

- **“I'm sorry to hear that (you've got a bad cold).”** — 听说你感冒得这么严重，我很遗憾。
- **“I hope you'll be all right very soon.”** — 希望你很快康复。
- **“Take extra care of yourself.”** — 多保重。
- **“That's too bad. What's the matter?”** — 真糟糕，怎么了？

> [!tip] Practical Point · 应用要点
> Showing concern for someone who is ill does not always require giving repeated advice.
>
> 对生病的人表示关心，不一定需要反复提出建议。

### 6.2 Changes in Communication Modes（交际方式的变化）

Modes of communication have changed dramatically in recent decades. The telephone, the Internet and personal communication devices (“smartphones”) have influenced **how, when, and why** people communicate. These trends have both benefits and disadvantages.

近几十年来，交际方式发生了显著变化。电话、互联网和个人通信设备（智能手机）改变了人们**如何、何时以及为何**进行交际。这些变化既带来益处，也存在不足。

### 6.3 Media Richness and Communication Effectiveness（媒介丰富度与交际有效性）

**Media Richness and Communication Effectiveness** are compared across different forms of communication, from the highest / most effective at the top to the lowest / least effective at the bottom.

**媒介丰富度与交际有效性**按照从高到低、从最有效到最不有效的方向比较不同交际形式。

| Order (High → Low) | Communication Methods（具体方式） | Form（交际形式） |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Conversation, Meeting（交谈、会议） | Face-to-Face（面对面） |
| 2 | Videoconferencing; IM or chat with video and audio（视频会议、带音视频的即时通信或聊天） | Video（视频） |
| 3 | Telephone（电话） | Audio（音频） |
| 4 | Blogs, Chat, Message boards, IM（博客、聊天、留言板、即时通信） | Written, Addressed（有明确接收对象的书面交流） |
| 5 | Letter, Memo, Note, E-mail（信件、备忘录、便条、电子邮件） | Written, Addressed（有明确接收对象的书面交流） |
| 6 | Spam, Newsletter, Flyer, Bulletin, Poster（群发信息、简讯、传单、公告、海报） | Unaddressed Documents（无特定接收对象的文件） |

### 6.4 Comparing Forms of Organizational Communication（组织交际形式比较）

#### A. Paper Based (Hard Copy)（纸质形式）

**Deliverables / Form:** Memos, letters, employee newsletters, brochures, performance appraisals, pay-packet enclosures, agendas and minutes, internal and external reports, questionnaires.

**呈现形式：** 备忘录、信件、员工简讯、宣传册、绩效评估、工资袋附带材料、议程与会议记录、内外部报告、调查问卷。

**Advantages:** Creates a permanent record; is convenient to distribute; may be economical; promotes comprehension and recall; allows precise and uniform expression; gives the audience flexibility in when and how to receive content.

**优点：** 形成永久记录；分发方便；可能较为经济；促进理解与记忆；表达精确且统一；接收者可以灵活安排接收内容的时间和方式。

**Disadvantages:** Leaves a paper trail; requires skill and effort to produce; lacks verbal cues and warmth; cannot be immediately adjusted to audience feedback; may seem impersonal.

**缺点：** 留下纸质记录；制作需要技巧与精力；缺乏言语线索与亲切感；无法根据受众反馈即时调整；可能显得缺乏个人色彩。

#### B. Digital (Soft Copy)（数字形式）

**Deliverables / Form:** E-mail, newsletters, brochures, instant messages, websites, intranets, blogs, social media posts, agendas and minutes, internal and external reports, questionnaires.

**呈现形式：** 电子邮件、简讯、宣传册、即时消息、网站、内部网络、博客、社交媒体帖子、议程与会议记录、内外部报告、调查问卷。

**Advantages:** In addition to all the advantages of paper output, it is more economical than paper, can be mass-produced and distributed, and allows mobile access anytime and anywhere.

**优点：** 除纸质输出的全部优点外，通常更经济，可批量制作和分发，支持随时随地通过移动设备访问。

**Disadvantages:** Leaves a permanent digital footprint; can lead to public embarrassment on a massive scale; may be inaccessible to unplugged audiences; is subject to malfunctions.

**缺点：** 留下持久的数字痕迹；可能大范围引发公开尴尬；未接入网络的受众可能无法访问；可能发生技术故障。

#### C. Oral（口头形式）

**Deliverables / Form:** Telephone calls, face-to-face conversations, in-person meetings, conferences, team addresses, seminars, workshops, training sessions, roundtables, teleconferences.

**呈现形式：** 电话、面对面交谈、线下会议、会议、团队讲话、研讨会、工作坊、培训、圆桌讨论、电话会议。

**Advantages:** Provides immediate feedback; can be adjusted to the audience; can be delivered quickly; supplies nonverbal cues; may create warm feelings; can be powerful.

**优点：** 提供即时反馈；能够根据受众调整；传递迅速；包含非语言线索；可能营造亲切感；具有较强影响力。

**Disadvantages:** Lacks a permanent record (unless recorded); may contain careless or imprecise expression; may be inappropriate for formal or complex ideas; does not promote easy recall.

**缺点：** 缺少永久记录（除非录音）；可能存在随意或不精确的表达；可能不适合正式或复杂的思想；不利于轻松回忆。

## 8. Culture and Communication（文化与交际）

### 8.1 Cultural Exchange: Confucianism in Japan（文化交流：儒家思想传入日本）

#### First Stage（第一阶段）

Around the end of the third century CE, Wang Ren from the Korean Peninsula introduced *The Analects* to Japan, where it soon received attention from the Japanese ruling class. Before the Sui and Tang periods, cultural exchanges between China and Japan had taken place through the Korean Peninsula. Only after the opening and establishment of the Maritime Silk Road following the Sui and Tang periods did more direct contacts and exchanges become possible. Therefore, the historical accounts of the introduction of *The Analects* are consistent with the circumstances of that time. The Japanese emperor was even said to have taken Wang Ren as his teacher in the hope of studying more Confucian thought. This is regarded as the earliest documented record of Confucian learning in Japanese history and reflects the pattern of cultural exchanges between China and Japan at the time. At this stage, only *The Analects* had been introduced. Although Confucian thought received attention from the ruling class, it had not yet exerted a broader influence on Japanese society. Its role was primarily that of an introduction, preparing the way for later development and further introduction of Confucian culture in Japan.

第一个阶段, 大约在公元三世纪末期, 当时朝鲜半岛的王仁将《论语》传入日本, 随后就得到了日本统治阶级的重视。在隋唐时期以前, 中日两国的文化交流, 都是通过朝鲜半岛而展开的, 一直到隋唐之后, 海上丝绸之路的开辟和确立, 才让中日两国有了更为直接的接触和交流。因此《论语》传入的有关历史记录, 符合当时的时代背景。当时的日本天皇甚至拜王仁为师, 希望可以深入学习关于儒学的更多思想。这是日本历史上有证可考的, 最早的儒学记载, 也符合当时中日两国文化交流的形态。这个时期儒学思想的传入, 仅只有一本《论语》而已, 虽然得到了统治阶级的重视, 但是在整个日本社会之中并未有更大的影响。可以说, 这个阶段的儒家思想, 只能算是传入, 是为将来儒家文化在日本的进一步发展和引入做了一个铺垫而已。

#### Second Stage（第二阶段）

The second stage began after the Sui and Tang periods. During the first stage, the Japanese emperor had already encountered the Confucian ideals of **benevolence, righteousness, propriety, wisdom, and trustworthiness** (仁、义、礼、智、信) and had undertaken certain reforms in Japan. After the maritime routes of the Sui and Tang periods were established, Japan repeatedly sent large groups of envoys to China, with more than a hundred people in each mission. These groups also included many students and monks, who became important bridges for cultural exchanges on multiple levels. The famous **Taika Reform** occurred during this period and was closely connected with the envoys returning from China. Having witnessed the prosperity of Sui and Tang China, these envoys helped advance the development of Confucian thought in Japan. Japan learned from China in many areas, including thought and institutions; Confucian ideas consequently spread throughout Japanese society and became integrated with its existing culture. This period also coincided with the formation of the Japanese samurai ethos. Its ideals of **loyalty, righteousness, and courage** (忠、义、勇) were described as reflecting the influence of Chinese Confucian culture.

第二个阶段, 即隋唐时期之后。第一个阶段中, 日本天皇已经领略到儒家思想中提倡的“仁、义、礼、智、信”, 并且在日本国展开了一定的改革, 在隋唐海上丝路贯通之后, 更是多次派遣大量使者前往中国, 每次数量都过百人。在这个使者队伍中, 同时还有大量的留学生和僧侣, 成为促进中日文化多层次展开的重要桥梁。日本著名的“大化改新”就是发生在这个时期, 从根本上看, 日本的这一举措可谓直接源于从中国归来的隋唐使。这些目睹了中国隋唐时期繁荣兴旺景象的隋唐史, 将儒家思想在日本的发展向前推动了一步。这个时期的日本, 在思想、制度等多方面都展开了以中国作为标杆的学习, 儒家思想也因此在整个日本社会环境中蔓延开来, 并且与日本既有的文化相融合。这一时期, 同样也是日本武士道精神的形成阶段, 因此, 日本武士道精髓“忠、义、勇”, 可以说渗透着中国儒学文化的内涵。

### 8.2 The Map and Journey Metaphor（地图与旅程的比喻）

**The relationship between culture and communication is compared to the relationship between a map and a journey.**

**文化与交际的关系可以比作地图与旅程的关系。**

**Reflection question:** How do you understand and interpret this simile?

**思考问题：** 应当如何理解和解释这一比喻？

### 8.3 The Relationship Between Culture and Communication（文化与交际的关系）

> **No communication is independent of the social environment within which it occurs.**

任何交际都无法脱离其发生的社会环境而独立存在。

> **Culture is communication; communication is culture.**

文化就是交际；交际就是文化。

> **Every country has its own way of saying things. The important thing is that which lies behind people's words.**

每个国家都有自己的表达方式，重要的是理解人们话语背后的意义。

**Culture and communication are inseparably linked: you can't have one without the other.** Culture gives meaning and provides the context for communication, and the ability to communicate allows us to act out our cultural values and to share our language and our culture.

**文化与交际密不可分，二者相互依存。** 文化赋予交际意义并为交际提供情境；交际能力则使我们能够表现自身的文化价值观，并分享语言与文化。

## 9. Barriers to Communication（交际障碍）

### 9.1 Nine Barriers to Communication（九种交际障碍）

| Barrier | English Explanation | 中文翻译 |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Physical** | Time, environment, comfort and needs, and physical medium (e.g., telephone or letter). | **物理障碍**：时间、环境、舒适程度、需求及通信媒介（如电话或信件）。 |
| **2. Cultural** | Ethnic, religious, and social differences. | **文化障碍**：民族、宗教和社会差异。 |
| **3. Perceptual** | Viewing what is said from your own mindset. | **知觉障碍**：以自身思维方式理解他人话语。 |
| **4. Motivational** | The listener's mental inertia. | **动机障碍**：听者的心理惰性。 |
| **5. Experiential** | Lack of similar life happenings. | **经验障碍**：缺乏相似的生活经历。 |
| **6. Emotional** | Personal feelings of the listener. | **情感障碍**：听者的个人情绪和感受。 |
| **7. Linguistic** | Different languages spoken by the speaker and listener, or use of vocabulary beyond the listener's comprehension. | **语言障碍**：使用不同语言，或使用听者无法理解的词汇。 |
| **8. Nonverbal** | Nonword messages. | **非语言障碍**：非词语形式的信息。 |
| **9. Competition** | The listener's ability to do other things rather than hear the communication. | **竞争性干扰**：听者同时进行其他活动而未专注于交际信息。 |

## 10. Intercultural Communication（跨文化交际）

### 10.1 Definitions of Intercultural Communication（跨文化交际的定义）

**Rogers and Steinfatt (1999):**

> Intercultural communication is the exchange of information between individuals who are “unalike culturally”.

跨文化交际是文化背景不同的个体之间的信息交换。

**Collier and Thomas (1998):**

> Intercultural communication is contact between persons who identify themselves as distinct from one another in cultural terms.

跨文化交际是指在文化身份方面认为彼此不同的人之间的接触与交流。

### 10.2 Intercultural Communication (IC) and Cross-cultural Communication (CC)

| Term | Definition | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| **Intercultural Communication (IC)** | Communication between people of different cultures. | **文化间交流**：不同文化背景的人之间进行的交际。 |
| **Cross-cultural Communication (CC)** | Comparison of communicative behavior between different cultures. | **跨文化对比**：比较不同文化中的交际行为。 |

**Scholars may use these two terms interchangeably.**

**学者也可能交替使用这两个术语。**

### 10.3 Major Barriers in Intercultural Communication（跨文化交际中的主要障碍）

1. **Language difference** — 语言差异。
2. **Nonverbal communication:** gestures, postures, facial expressions, etc. — **非语言交际差异**：手势、姿势、面部表情等。
3. **Stereotypes:** culture, religion, ideas, values, etc. — **刻板印象**：与文化、宗教、思想、价值观等有关的固有印象。

## 11. Intercultural Case Studies and Attitudes（跨文化案例与交际态度）

### 11.1 Letters（书信案例）

**Discussion questions（讨论问题）：**

1. **Why did the students laugh?** — 学生们为什么笑？
2. **Why did the student say “Let's be friends”?** — 那名学生为什么说“我们交个朋友吧”？

### 11.2 Grandparents and Grandchildren（祖父母与孙辈）

**Discussion questions（讨论问题）：**

1. **Could you understand why Mike called the police?** — 你能理解 Mike 为什么报警吗？
2. **What can we learn about American culture and Chinese culture?** — 我们能从中了解中美文化的哪些特点？
3. **Why couldn't Xue Suling carry out her original plan of Chinese language teaching?** — 薛素玲为什么无法按原计划进行中文教学？
4. **What can we learn about American culture and Chinese culture?** — 这一经历揭示了中美文化的哪些差异？

### 11.3 A Famous Motto（重要格言）

> **All people have the right to be equal and the equal right to be different.**

**所有人都有平等的权利，也同样拥有保持差异的平等权利。**

### 11.4 The Correct Attitude Toward Cultural Differences（对待文化差异的正确态度）

**Adapted from L. M. Barna, “Stumbling Blocks in Intercultural Communication” (1992):**

> The communicator cannot stop at knowing that the people he is working with have different customs, goals, and thought patterns from his own. … He must be able to work with them and within them, neither losing his own values in the confrontation nor protecting himself behind a wall of intellectual detachment.

交际者不能仅仅停留在知道交往对象具有不同的习俗、目标和思维模式这一层面。他还必须能够与这些人共同合作，并融入他们的交往环境；既不能在文化碰撞中失去自己的价值观，也不能躲在理智疏离的壁垒之后保护自己。

### 11.5 Two Habits to Cultivate（应培养的两种习惯）

**1. Consciously make an effort to avoid stereotypes.**

**有意识地努力避免刻板印象。**

**2. Build the habit of making a conscious effort not to judge other cultures too quickly.**

**养成不轻率判断其他文化的习惯。**
