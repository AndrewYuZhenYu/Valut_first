# Chapter 4 — Cultural Value Orientations

**Intercultural Communication｜跨文化交际 · 中英双语知识笔记**

## 1. Cultural Patterns（文化模式）

### 1.1 Definition（定义）

> **Cultural Pattern, also called value orientation, is a term used to collectively discuss values, beliefs, other orientations that characterize the dominant group within a culture.**

**文化模式**又称**价值取向**，是用于统称某一文化中主导群体所具有的价值观、信仰及其他取向的术语。

Different cultural patterns characterize Chinese and Western cultures respectively.

不同的文化模式是中西文化差异的重要体现。

### 1.2 Components of Cultural Patterns（文化模式的构成）

- **Thinking Model** — 思维模式
- **Values** — 价值观
- **Norms** — 规范
- **Social Practices** — 社会实践

#### A. The Mode of Thinking（思维模式）

> Human thinking mainly consists of such elements as knowledge, ideology, methodology, intelligence, emotion, willpower, language and habits. The inter-relationship and interaction of these elements form a dynamic complex system known as the mode of thinking.

人类思维主要由知识、观念、方法、智力、情感、意志、语言和习惯等要素组成。这些要素相互联系、相互作用，形成一个动态而复杂的系统，即**思维模式**。

**Thinking Model: Major Differences（主要差异）**

| Westerner（西方人） | Chinese（中国人） |
|---|---|
| Rational Thinking（理性思维） | Intuitive Thinking（感性思维） |
| Analytical Thinking（分析思维） | Holistic Thinking（整体思维） |
| Objective Thinking（客观性思维） | Subjective Thinking（主观性思维） |
| Linear Thinking（线性思维） | Curvilinear Thinking（螺旋型思维） |

**Linear / Curvilinear Thinking（线性思维与螺旋型思维）**

Oriental thinking mode is described as circular or curvilinear, while the thinking mode of native English speakers is described as linear. Chinese discourse may introduce background first, develop the topic indirectly, and then reach its central point.

东方思维方式被描述为环形或螺旋型，英语母语者的思维方式被描述为线性。汉语语篇往往先介绍背景，再通过迂回阐述逐步引出中心主题。

#### B. Values（价值观）

> **Value refers to a principle, standard, or quality considered worthwhile or desirable.**

**价值观**是指被认为值得追求或令人向往的原则、标准或品质。

**Characteristics（特征）**：

- **The core of all the cultures** — 一切文化的核心。
- **A guide to people in a culture** — 文化群体成员的行为指导。
- **Applied in every aspect in the life** — 贯穿生活的各个方面。
- **It sets norms** — 设定行为规范。

**Value as the Core of Culture: The Peeling of an Onion（洋葱模型）**

| Layer（层次） | Meaning（含义） |
|---|---|
| **Symbols（符号）** | The most superficial and easiest to perceive: words, pictures, gestures, objects. 表层、最容易感知：词语、图片、手势、物品。 |
| **Heroes（英雄人物）** | Famous or ordinary, real or imaginary persons. 著名或普通、真实或虚构的人物。 |
| **Rituals（仪式）** | Socially essential collective activities, such as festivals, weddings and funerals. 被社会视为必要的集体活动，如节日、婚礼和葬礼。 |
| **Values（价值观）** | The core of culture; social principles, goals and standards. 文化的核心，包含社会原则、目标和标准。 |

**The Core Value of Traditional Chinese Culture（中国传统文化的核心价值）**

> Benevolence is the core value of traditional Chinese culture.

**仁（Benevolence）**是中国传统文化的核心价值。相关的五项德目为 **Benevolence, Righteousness, Manner, Wisdom, Credit（仁、义、礼、智、信）**。

### 1.3 Types of Cultural Patterns（文化模式的理论分类）

Three representative approaches are **Hofstede's Value Dimensions**, **Kluckhohn and Strodtbeck's Classification of Value Orientations**, and **Edward Hall's High-Context and Low-Context Orientation**.

三种代表性的文化模式研究体系是**霍夫斯泰德的价值维度**、**克拉克洪与斯多特贝克的价值取向分类**和**霍尔的高、低语境取向**。

The following five orientations belong to **Kluckhohn and Strodtbeck's model**.

以下五个价值取向属于**克拉克洪与斯多特贝克模型**。

## 2. Kluckhohn and Strodtbeck's Value Orientation Model（价值取向模型）

### 2.1 Five Basic Questions（五个基本问题）

1. **What is the character of innate human nature?** — 人的先天本性是什么？（Human Nature Orientation）
2. **What is the relation of man to nature?** — 人与自然是什么关系？（Person-Nature Orientation）
3. **What is the temporal focus?** — 时间关注的重点是什么？（Time Orientation）
4. **What is the mode of human activity?** — 人类活动的方式是什么？（Activity Orientation）
5. **What is the mode of human relationship?** — 人与人如何建立关系？（Relational / Social Orientation）

### 2.2 Human Nature Orientation（人性取向）

Three views of human nature are distinguished:

人性取向分为三种观点：

1. **Humans are evil but perfectible.** — 人性本恶，但可以改善至完善。
2. **Humans are a mixture of good and evil.** — 人性兼有善与恶。
3. **Humans are good but corruptible.** — 人性本善，但可能受到腐化。

#### (1) Humans Are Evil but Perfectible（本恶但可完善）

> The traditional Western belief about human nature is that human beings are basically evil.

传统西方人性观念认为，人基本上是恶的。

**Associated cultures / beliefs（对应文化与信仰）：Puritans（清教徒）、Christianity（基督教）。**

- **Original sin（原罪）**: According to Christian teaching, humans are born with original sin. 根据基督教教义，人带有原罪出生。
- **Salvation and improvement（救赎与改善）**: People can be saved by God and seek to perfect their nature by doing good things. 人可以通过上帝获得救赎，并通过行善改善自身本性。
- **Guilt culture（罪感文化）**: Confessing wrongdoing is associated with the process of becoming good. 承认过错与趋向善的过程相联系。

**Example（例子）:** Adam and Eve committed the original sin when they ate the forbidden fruit in the Garden of Eden.

亚当与夏娃在伊甸园偷吃禁果，犯下原罪。

#### (2) Humans Are a Mixture of Good and Evil（善恶兼有）

> Everybody has both good side and bad side.

每个人都有善的一面，也有恶的一面。

**Associated culture（对应文化）：Europeans（欧洲人）。**

The distrust of human nature is reflected in American political institutions with **“checks and balances”**: government branches and media journalists monitor officials to prevent wrongdoing.

对人性的戒备体现在美国政治制度的**制衡原则（checks and balances）**中：政府部门与新闻媒体监督公职人员，以防止不当行为。

#### (3) Humans Are Good but Corruptible（本善但可腐化）

**Associated ideas and cultures（对应观念与文化）：Confucian teaching in China and Asian countries（中国及亚洲国家的儒家思想）。**

- People are believed to be basically good; children are pure and innocent. 人被认为本性基本善良，儿童纯真无邪。
- People may become corrupt as they grow older and have more contact with society. 随年龄增长及社会接触增多，人可能受到腐化。
- Parents, teachers, and political leaders are responsible for protecting morality and serving as **models of virtue**. 父母、教师和政治领导者负有维护道德并以身作则的责任。

**Examples（例子）:** “人之初，性本善” and **Mencius' mother moving three times（孟母三迁）**。

**Shame culture（耻感文化）** is associated with China; **guilt culture（罪感文化）** is associated with the United States. Japan is also mentioned in the discussion of moral judgment and shame.

**耻感文化**与中国相关，**罪感文化**与美国相关；日本也出现在关于道德评判与羞耻的讨论中。

> [!important] Human Nature — Cultural Associations（人性取向与文化归属）
> | Basic Value | Associated Cultures |
> |---|---|
> | **Basically Good（基本善）** | **Native Americans, Hindu（美洲原住民、印度教）** |
> | **Mixture of Good and Evil（善恶兼有）** | **Europeans（欧洲人）** |
> | **Basically Evil（基本恶）** | **Puritans, Christianity（清教徒、基督教）** |
>
> “Basically Good” and “Humans are good but corruptible” are retained as separately worded classifications rather than silently treated as identical.
>
> “基本善”与“本善但可腐化”保留各自原有的分类措辞，不擅自将二者改成同一个定义。

### 2.3 Person-Nature Orientation（人与自然关系取向）

Three orientations describe the relationship between humans and nature:

人与自然关系包括三种取向：

| Orientation（取向） | Cultural Associations（文化归属） | Central View（核心观念） |
|---|---|---|
| **Subjugation to Nature（屈从自然）** | **India, parts of South America, Native Americans**（印度、南美部分地区、美洲原住民） | Nature controls humans; powerful natural forces must be accepted. 自然支配人类，人必须接受强大的自然力量。 |
| **Harmony with Nature（与自然和谐）** | **East Asia: China, Japan, Thailand; Native Americans**（东亚：中国、日本、泰国；美洲原住民） | Humans should live in harmony with nature. 人应当与自然和谐相处。 |
| **Mastery over Nature（控制自然）** | **North America; Western approach**（北美；西方取向） | Humans seek to control or change nature. 人力求控制或改变自然。 |

#### (1) Subjugation to Nature（屈从自然）

> The most powerful forces of life are beyond their control. Whether the force be a god, fate, or magic, a person cannot overcome it and must therefore learn to accept it.

生命中最强大的力量不受个人控制，无论这种力量是神、命运还是魔法，人都无法克服它，因而必须学会接受。

Earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, and floods are examples of natural forces associated with this orientation.

地震、火山喷发和洪水是这一取向所涉及的自然力量的例子。

#### (2) Harmony with Nature（与自然和谐）

**Key idea:** Live according to the rhythms of nature and maintain balance.

**核心观念：** 顺应自然规律，保持和谐与平衡。

Examples include **天人感应**（Nature affects human affairs and human behavior finds response in Nature）and **天从人愿**（Nature accords with human wishes）。

相关表达包括**天人感应**和**天从人愿**。

**Chinese gardens** integrate pavilions, paths, water, trees and rocks; **traditional Chinese medicine** treats the human body as part of nature and emphasizes restoring balance.

**中国园林**将亭台、道路、水体、树木和山石融为一体；**中医**将人体视为自然的一部分，并强调恢复平衡。

#### (3) Mastery over Nature（控制自然）

> This value orientation is characteristic of the Western approach, which has a long tradition of valuing technology, change, and science.

这一价值取向具有西方文化的特征，体现了重视技术、变化和科学的传统。

People with this orientation see a separation between humans and nature and believe problems can be solved or systems changed through knowledge and control.

这一取向将人与自然相区分，认为人可以通过知识和控制解决问题、改变系统。

### 2.4 Time Orientation（时间取向）

Time orientation has three types: **Past Orientation, Present Orientation, and Future Orientation**.

时间取向分为**过去取向、现在取向、未来取向**三种。

#### (1) Past-oriented Culture（过去取向文化）

> In the past Oriented cultures, history, established religions, and tradition are extremely important.

在过去取向文化中，历史、既有宗教及传统极其重要。

**Cultural examples（文化实例）:** **China, Japan, the United Kingdom, France**; **Belgium** is also identified as respecting heritage, history and tradition.

**代表国家：中国、日本、英国、法国；比利时也被列为重视文化遗产、历史和传统的国家。**

**Chinese orientation（中国文化取向）:** Tradition is important; time may be experienced as cyclical, and new events are often understood by reference to the past.

**中国文化特点：** 重视传统，可能以循环的方式理解时间，并通过过去的经验认识新事物。

#### (2) Present-oriented Culture（现在取向文化）

> Present-oriented culture hold that the immediate moment carries the most significance.

现在取向文化认为**当下时刻**具有最大的意义。

**Cultural examples（文化实例）:** **Philippines / Filipinos, Spain, Latin Americans, African Americans**.

**代表文化：菲律宾／菲律宾人、西班牙、拉丁美洲人、非裔美国人。**

The future is seen as ambiguous and beyond individual control; greater emphasis is placed on enjoying the moment, spontaneity, and a relaxed lifestyle.

未来被视为具有不确定性，且不完全受个人控制；更强调享受当下、自发性与轻松的生活方式。

#### (3) Future-oriented Culture（未来取向文化）

> Future-Oriented culture value the future. taking chances, a stress on youth, and optimism are all hallmarks of cultures that hold this orientation.

未来取向文化重视未来，冒险精神、重视青年和乐观态度是其典型特征。

**Cultural example（文化实例）: Dominant U.S. / American culture（美国主导文化）。**

A strong belief in progress encourages planning and goal-setting. Time is treated as linear; **“Time is money.”** The Puritans placed special emphasis on not wasting time and planning for the future.

对进步的强烈信念促使人们制定计划和目标；时间被视为线性的，强调**“时间就是金钱”**。清教徒尤其重视避免浪费时间以及为未来制订计划。

> [!important] Time Orientation — Complete Cultural Examples（时间取向文化对应表）
> | Past-oriented（过去） | Present-oriented（现在） | Future-oriented（未来） |
> |---|---|---|
> | **China, Japan, UK, France; Belgium** | **Philippines, Spain, Latin Americans, African Americans** | **United States (dominant culture)** |

### 2.5 Activity Orientation（活动取向）

> **Activity orientation refers to how people of a culture view activity.**

**活动取向**指一个文化群体如何看待活动。

Three orientations are distinguished: **Doing**, **Being**, and **Being-in-Becoming**.

活动取向分为**做事取向、存在取向、成为取向**。

#### (1) Doing Orientation（做事取向）

> The doing-oriented culture stresses activity and action.

做事取向文化强调**活动与行动**。

**Associated culture（对应文化）: Dominant American / U.S. culture（美国主导文化）。**

- Focus on externally measurable accomplishments. 强调可以从外部衡量的成就。
- Planning, problem-solving, decisions, deadlines, and schedules are important. 重视计划、解决问题、决策、期限与日程。
- **“What do you do?” — “I design software.”** 侧重一个人在做什么工作。

#### (2) Being Orientation（存在取向）

**Associated cultures（对应文化）: Latino cultures; Mexican examples（拉丁美洲文化；墨西哥人的实例）。**

- Stress **who you are** rather than only what you accomplish. 强调**你是谁**，而不仅是完成了什么。
- Accept people, events and ideas as they occur; emphasize spontaneity and enjoying the moment. 接纳自然发生的人、事和观念，强调随性与享受当下。
- Value interpersonal relations; social status, positions and roles can be significant. 重视人际关系，社会地位、身份及角色具有重要意义。
- **“What do you do?” — “I am a manager.”** 强调身份。

#### (3) Being-in-Becoming Orientation（成为取向）

> Being-in-Becoming culture stresses the idea of development and growth.

成为取向文化强调**发展与成长**。

**Associated traditions（对应传统）: Hinduism and Buddhism（印度教与佛教）。**

The goal is internal rather than externally measurable: development of the whole person, as in certain forms of Eastern meditation.

其目标是内在的，而非能够从外部直接衡量的成就，例如通过某些东方冥想方式促进整个人的全面发展。

### 2.6 Relational Orientation（人际关系取向）

Three relational orientations are **Hierarchy**, **Group**, and **Individual**.

人际关系取向包括**等级取向、群体取向和个人取向**。

| Orientation（取向） | Cultural Examples（文化实例） | Key Characteristics（核心特征） |
|---|---|---|
| **Hierarchy（等级取向）** | **China（中国）** | Obey authority; know your place; treat others according to position; look to leading figures. 服从权威、明确自身地位、按照身份对待他人、参考领导者的意见。 |
| **Group（群体取向）** | **Native Americans（美洲原住民）** | Respond to others' feelings; mutual dependence; loyalty; consult the group. 关注他人感受、相互依赖、忠诚、重视群体意见。 |
| **Individual（个人取向）** | **United States（美国）** | Express your own ideas; independence and self-reliance; make your own choices. 表达个人观点、独立自主、自行决策。 |

**Hierarchy in Chinese Values（中国文化中的等级取向）**

Society consists of hierarchies; a person may be at the top of one hierarchy but below others in a larger hierarchy. Position may depend on **age, family social position, or academic achievement**.

社会由不同的等级关系构成；一个人可能在某一等级关系中处于高位，却在更大的等级体系中处于低位。地位可能由**年龄、家庭社会地位或学业成就**等因素决定。

## 3. Limitations and Conclusion（模型局限与结论）

### 3.1 Limitations of Kluckhohn's Model（模型的局限）

> 1. Not everyone in a culture has the same basic values.
> 2. Cultures change over time.
> 3. The concept of basic values is itself a generalization.

1. 同一文化中的人并不一定具有相同的基本价值观。
2. 文化会随着时间变化。
3. 基本价值观的概念本身就是一种概括。

### 3.2 Conclusion（结论）

Within any culture, most people tend to prefer a particular set of solutions, but individuals may choose different alternatives. Cultural patterns describe broad tendencies, not identical beliefs held by every member.

在任何文化中，大多数人往往倾向于某一组解决方式，但个体可能作出其他选择。文化模式描述的是整体倾向，并不表示每个成员都有完全一致的观念。

### 3.3 Case Study: Caring for the Elderly（案例：照顾老年人）

**Situation:** An American teacher's 83-year-old grandmother lives alone, while her children have moved away.

**情境：** 一位美国教师的祖母已经 83 岁，独自居住，子女都已离家。

**Individualist culture:** Taking aged parents into one's home may be seen as an economic burden or a threat to household privacy and independence. Caring for elderly parents also includes respecting their **autonomy and independence**.

**个人主义文化：** 将年迈父母接到家中同住，可能被视为经济负担或对家庭隐私和独立性的影响。关爱老年父母也包括尊重他们的**自主权与独立性**。

## 4. Five Value Orientations — Quick Reference（五种价值取向速查）

| Orientation（取向） | Alternatives（主要分类） |
|---|---|
| **1. Human Nature** 人性取向 | Evil but perfectible（本恶但可完善） / Mixture of good and evil（善恶兼有） / Good but corruptible（本善但可腐化） |
| **2. Person–Nature** 人与自然取向 | Subjugation（屈从自然） / Harmony（与自然和谐） / Mastery（控制自然） |
| **3. Time** 时间取向 | Past（过去） / Present（现在） / Future（未来） |
| **4. Activity** 活动取向 | Doing（做事） / Being（存在） / Being-in-Becoming（成为） |
| **5. Relational** 人际关系取向 | Hierarchy（等级） / Group（群体） / Individual（个人） |
