## 1. Cultural Patterns（文化模式）

### 1.1 Definition（定义）

> **Cultural Pattern, also called value orientation, is a term used to collectively discuss values, beliefs, other orientations that characterize the dominant group within a culture.**

**文化模式（Cultural Pattern）**又称**价值取向（Value Orientation）**，用于统称某一文化中主导群体所具有的价值观、信仰及其他取向。

Different cultural patterns characterize Chinese, Westerners and their cultures respectively.

不同的文化模式体现了中国人、西方人及其文化之间的差异。

### 1.2 Components of Cultural Patterns（文化模式的构成）

| Component | 中文 | Key Point / 核心含义 |
|---|---|---|
| **Thinking Model** | 思维模式 | Patterns of thinking and reasoning. 思考与推理的方式。 |
| **Values** | 价值观 | What is considered worthwhile or desirable. 被认为有价值或值得追求的事物。 |
| **Norms** | 规范 | Standards guiding behavior. 指导行为的准则。 |
| **Social Practices** | 社会实践 | Activities and practices in social life. 社会生活中的活动与实践。 |

#### A. The Mode of Thinking（思维模式）

> Human thinking mainly consists of such elements as knowledge, ideology, methodology, intelligence, emotion, willpower, language and habits. The inter-relationship and interaction of these elements form a dynamic complex system known as the mode of thinking.

人类思维主要由**知识、观念、方法、智力、情感、意志、语言和习惯**等八大要素组成。这些要素相互联系、相互作用，形成一个动态复杂的系统，即**思维模式**。

**Thinking Model: Major Differences（思维模式的主要差异）**

| Westerner（西方人） | Chinese（中国人） |
|---|---|
| **Rational Thinking**（理性思维） | **Intuitive Thinking**（感性思维） |
| **Analytical Thinking**（分析思维） | **Holistic Thinking**（整体思维） |
| **Objective Thinking**（客观性思维） | **Subjective Thinking**（主观性思维） |
| **Linear Thinking**（线性思维） | **Curvilinear Thinking**（螺旋型思维） |

**Linear and Curvilinear Thinking（线性与螺旋型思维）**

> This diagram shows that oriental thinking mode is circular or curvilinear while the thinking mode of the native English speakers is linear.

东方人的思维模式被描述为环形或螺旋型，而英语母语者的思维模式被描述为线性。

> With this type of thinking mode, the Chinese sentences are usually of end weight and the sequence of time and place is arranged from the general to the specific.

在这种思维模式下，汉语句子通常呈现**末端重心**的特点，时间与地点的表达顺序通常由一般到具体。

In Chinese discourse, background and the significance of a topic may be introduced before the central point is stated. The discussion develops in a circular or indirect manner and gradually approaches the main idea.

在汉语语篇中，文章开头常先说明主题的意义或背景，再采用迂回铺陈的方式逐步引出中心主题；这一特点也与委婉、含蓄的表达方式相联系。

#### B. Values（价值观）

> **Value refers to a principle, standard, or quality considered worthwhile or desirable.**

**价值**是指被认为值得追求或令人向往的**原则、标准或品质**。

**Characteristics of Values（价值观的特征）**

| English | 中文翻译 |
|---|---|
| **The core of all the cultures** | 一切文化的核心 |
| **A guide to people in a culture** | 为文化群体中的人们提供指导 |
| **Applied in every aspect in the life** | 应用于生活的各个方面 |
| **It sets norms** | 设立行为规范 |

**Value as the Core of Culture — The Peeling of an Onion（价值是文化的核心——洋葱模型）**

Geert Hofstede compares culture to an onion: symbols, heroes and rituals form layers surrounding the core of values.

霍夫斯泰德将文化比作洋葱：**符号、英雄人物、仪式**等层次包围着核心的**价值观**。

| Layer（层次） | English Explanation | 中文翻译 |
|---|---|---|
| **Symbols（符号）** | The most superficial and the easiest to perceive; words, pictures, gestures and objects. | 最表层、最容易察觉；包括词语、图片、手势和物品。 |
| **Heroes（英雄人物）** | Famous or ordinary, real or imaginary persons. | 著名或普通、真实或虚构的人物。 |
| **Rituals（仪式）** | Collective activities considered socially essential, such as religious and cultural practices, festivals, weddings and funerals. | 被社会视为必要的集体活动，包括宗教文化活动、节日、婚礼和葬礼。 |
| **Values（价值观）** | The core of culture. | 文化的核心。 |

**The Core Value of Traditional Chinese Culture（中国传统文化的核心价值）**

> Benevolence is the core value of traditional Chinese culture.

**仁（Benevolence）**是中国传统文化的核心价值。

The five traditional virtues are **Benevolence, Righteousness, Manner, Wisdom, Credit**.

五项传统德目是**仁、义、礼、智、信**。

### 1.3 Approaches to Cultural Patterns（文化模式的研究体系）

Three theoretical approaches are presented:

文化模式主要涉及三种理论研究体系：

1. **Kluckhohn and Strodtbeck's Value Orientation** — 克拉克洪与斯多特贝克的价值取向理论。
2. **Hofstede's Dimensions of Cultural Variability** — 霍夫斯泰德的文化差异维度，包括 Individualism and Collectivism（个人主义与集体主义）、Uncertainty Avoidance（不确定性规避）、Power Distance（权力距离）、Masculinity–Femininity（男性气质—女性气质）。
3. **Edward T. Hall's Context–Culture Theory** — 爱德华·霍尔的语境文化理论，包括 High-context（高语境）和 Low-context（低语境）。

The following model focuses on **five value orientations**.

以下重点学习的是**五种价值取向**。

## 2. Kluckhohn and Strodtbeck's Value Orientation Model（克拉克洪与斯多特贝克价值取向模型）

### 2.1 Five Basic Questions（五个基本问题）

Five basic questions need answering at the root of any culture:

每一种文化都需要回答五个基本问题：

| Basic Question（原有问题） | Orientation（取向） | 中文翻译 |
|---|---|---|
| **What is the character of innate human nature?** | Human Nature Orientation | 人的先天本性是什么？——人性取向 |
| **What is the relation of man to nature?** | Person-Nature Orientation | 人与自然的关系是什么？——人与自然取向 |
| **What is the temporal focus?** | Time Orientation | 时间关注的重点是什么？——时间取向 |
| **What is the mode of human activity?** | Activity Orientation | 人类活动采用什么方式？——活动取向 |
| **What is the mode of human relationship?** | Relational Orientation | 人际关系采用什么模式？——人际关系取向 |

### 2.2 Human Nature Orientation（人性取向）

Three views of human nature are distinguished:

人性取向包括以下三种观点：

1. **Humans are evil but perfectible.** — 人性本恶，但可以改善至完善。
2. **Humans are a mixture of good and evil.** — 人性兼有善与恶。
3. **Humans are good but corruptible.** — 人性本善，但可能受到腐化。

#### (1) Humans Are Evil but Perfectible（本恶但可完善）

> The traditional Western belief about human nature is that human beings are basically evil.

传统西方人性观念认为，人基本上是恶的。

**Associated beliefs / cultures（相关信仰与文化）：Christianity（基督教）; Puritans（清教徒）。**

- **Original sin:** According to Christian teaching, humans are born with original sin.  
  **原罪：** 根据基督教教义，人带着原罪出生。
- **Salvation:** Human beings can be saved from evil by God.  
  **救赎：** 人能够通过上帝的救赎摆脱罪恶。
- **Perfectibility:** Human nature can be improved, even perfected in time, through doing good.  
  **可完善性：** 人可以通过行善改善自身本性，甚至逐渐趋于完善。
- **Guilt culture:** Confessing the bad one has done is the first step in becoming good.  
  **罪感文化：** 承认自身过错被视为走向善的第一步。

**Example（例子）**

> Adam and Eve committed the original sin when they ate the forbidden fruit in the Garden of Eden.

亚当与夏娃在伊甸园偷吃禁果，犯下了原罪。

#### (2) Humans Are a Mixture of Good and Evil（善恶兼有）

> Everybody has both good side and bad side.

每个人既有善的一面，也有恶的一面。

**Associated culture（对应文化）：Europeans（欧洲人）。**

The distrust of human nature is reflected in American political institutions with **“checks and balances.”** The legislative branch and media journalists monitor government officials to prevent wrongdoing.

对人性的戒备体现在美国政治制度的**“制衡原则”（checks and balances）**中：立法部门与媒体记者监督政府官员，以防止不当行为。

#### (3) Humans Are Good but Corruptible（本善但可腐化）

> Human beings draw close to one another by their common nature, but habits and customs keep them apart. — Confucian saying

**儒家思想：** 人们因共同的本性而相近，但因后天习惯与风俗而产生差异。

**Associated culture（对应文化）：Chinese values（中国文化价值观），especially Confucian teaching（尤其是儒家思想）。**

- **Basically good:** Children are pure and innocent.  
  **本性基本善良：** 儿童纯真无邪。
- **Corruptible:** People may become corrupt while growing older and having more contact with society.  
  **可能受到腐化：** 随着年龄增长和社会接触增多，人可能受到不良影响。
- **Models of virtue:** Parents, teachers and political leaders should protect the morality of those under their care and serve as moral examples.  
  **道德榜样：** 父母、教师和政治领导者应维护所照顾者的道德，并以身作则。

**Examples（例子）：** **“人之初，性本善”**（The original goodness of human nature）; **Mencius' mother moving three times（孟母三迁）**。

**Guilt Culture and Shame Culture（罪感文化与耻感文化）**

- **Guilt culture — United States:** Moral judgment is associated with guilt and the distinction between right and wrong.  
  **罪感文化——美国：** 道德评判与罪恶感、是非对错相联系。
- **Shame culture — China (Japan is also mentioned):** Moral judgment is associated with shame and social criticism of improper conduct.  
  **耻感文化——中国（也涉及日本）：** 道德评判与羞耻感及社会对不当行为的道德批判相联系。

> [!important] Human Nature — Cultural Associations（人性取向与文化归属）
> | Basic Value | Cultures / 中文 |
> |---|---|
> | **Basically Good（基本善）** | **Native Americans, Hindu**（美洲原住民、印度教） |
> | **Mixture of Good and Evil（善恶兼有）** | **Europeans**（欧洲人） |
> | **Basically Evil（基本恶）** | **Puritans, Christianity**（清教徒、基督教） |
>
> *“Basically Good / Basically Evil” are the labels used in the comparative classification; “good but corruptible / evil but perfectible” are retained as distinct explanatory formulations.*  
> “基本善／基本恶”是文化比较表中的分类名称；“本善但可腐化／本恶但可完善”则是相应内容的解释性表述，**保留原有措辞，不擅自混同**。

### 2.3 Person-Nature Orientation（人与自然关系取向）

Three orientations describe the relationship between human beings and nature.

人与自然关系取向分为三种：

| Orientation | Associated Cultures | 中文翻译 |
|---|---|---|
| **Subjugation to Nature** | India; parts of South America; Native Americans | **屈从自然**：印度、南美部分地区、美洲原住民 |
| **Harmony with Nature** | East Asia: China, Japan, Thailand; Native Americans | **与自然和谐**：东亚（中国、日本、泰国）、美洲原住民 |
| **Mastery over Nature** | North America; Western approach | **控制自然**：北美、西方取向 |

#### (1) Subjugation to Nature（屈从自然）

> The most powerful forces of life are beyond their control. Whether the force be a god, fate, or magic, a person cannot overcome it and must therefore learn to accept it.

生命中最强大的力量不受个人控制。无论这种力量是神、命运还是魔法，人都无法克服，因此必须学会接受。

The best way to deal with nature is to respect it and act humbly before powerful external forces. Natural disasters such as earthquakes, volcanic eruptions and floods illustrate this belief.

应对自然的方式是**尊重自然，并在强大的外部力量面前保持谦逊**。地震、火山喷发和洪水等自然灾害体现了自然力量的强大。

**Key ideas（关键词）：** Accept your fate（接受命运）; Life is outside the individual's control（生活不完全受个人控制）; Be humble（保持谦逊）。

#### (2) Harmony with Nature（与自然和谐）

Humans should live according to the rhythms of nature and maintain balance.

人应该**顺应自然规律，与自然保持平衡**。

**Expressions（相关表达）：**

- **Nature affects human affairs and human behavior finds response in Nature** — 天人感应。
- **The law of Nature and the feelings of humanity are in unison** — 天理人情。
- **Nature accords with human wishes** — 天从人愿。

**Examples（例子）**

- **Chinese gardens:** Pavilions and paved pathways are integrated with water, trees and rocks.  
  **中国园林：** 亭台、道路与水体、树木、山石等自然要素融为一体。
- **Traditional Chinese medicine:** The human body is part of nature and needs to be brought back to balance.  
  **中医：** 人体是自然的一部分，需要恢复协调与平衡。

#### (3) Mastery over Nature（控制自然）

> This value orientation is characteristic of the Western approach, which has a long tradition of valuing technology, change, and science.

这种价值取向具有西方文化的特征，体现了重视**技术、变化和科学**的传统。

People with this orientation see a clear separation between humans and nature. They believe that by understanding and controlling the environment, they can improve productivity, security and comfort.

持这种取向的人将人类与自然明确区分，认为通过认识和控制环境，可以提高生产效率，获得物质保障和舒适生活。

**Key ideas（关键词）：** Make life comfortable and convenient（使生活舒适便利）; Problems can be solved once we know the facts（掌握事实后就能解决问题）; Be objective（保持客观）。

**Comparison（简明对照）**

| Subjugation | Harmony | Mastery |
|---|---|---|
| Accept nature's power. 接受自然的力量。 | Live in balance with nature. 与自然保持平衡。 | Control and change nature. 控制并改变自然。 |

### 2.4 Time Orientation（时间取向）

Time orientation includes **Past Orientation, Present Orientation and Future Orientation**.

时间取向包括**过去取向、现在取向和未来取向**。

#### (1) Past-oriented Culture（过去取向文化）

> In the past Oriented cultures, history, established religions, and tradition are extremely important.

在过去取向文化中，**历史、既有宗教和传统极其重要**。

**Cultural examples（文化实例）：China, Japan, UK, France; Belgium.**  
**代表国家：中国、日本、英国、法国；比利时也被列为重视历史、传统和文化遗产的国家。**

Respect for the past is especially evident in many Asian nations because of their long histories. Great Britain, France and Belgium also tend to revere their heritage and traditions.

由于悠久的历史，许多亚洲国家尤其重视过去；英国、法国和比利时也倾向于尊重自身的文化遗产和传统。

**Chinese values（中国文化价值观）**

- **Tradition is important.** — 重视传统。
- **Something new feels more secure when connected to the past.** — 当新事物能够与过去的经历相联系时，人们更有安全感。
- **Time is cyclical.** — 时间被理解为周期性循环。

#### (2) Present-oriented Culture（现在取向文化）

> Present-oriented culture hold that the immediate moment carries the most significance.

现在取向文化认为，**当下时刻具有最大的意义**。

**Cultural examples（文化实例）：Filipinos / Philippines, Spain, Latin Americans, African Americans.**  
**代表文化：菲律宾人／菲律宾、西班牙、拉丁美洲人、非裔美国人。**

The future is seen as ambiguous and, in a sense, beyond individual control. Greater importance is placed on enjoying the moment, spontaneity and a relaxed lifestyle.

未来被视为不确定的，在某种意义上超出个人控制；因此更强调**享受当下、自发性和轻松的生活方式**。

#### (3) Future-oriented Culture（未来取向文化）

> Future-Oriented culture value the future. taking chances, a stress on youth, and optimism are all hallmarks of cultures that hold this orientation.

未来取向文化重视未来。**冒险、重视青年和乐观精神**是这种取向的典型特征。

**Cultural example（文化实例）：American culture / U.S. dominant culture（美国文化／美国主导文化）。**

A strong belief in progress encourages people to make plans, set goals and try new things. Time is viewed as linear: **“Time is money.”** The Puritans emphasized avoiding wasted time and planning for the future.

对进步的强烈信念鼓励人们制定计划、设立目标并尝试新事物。时间被视为线性的，强调**“时间就是金钱”**。清教徒尤其强调避免浪费时间及规划未来。

> [!important] Time Orientation — Cultural Examples（时间取向与文化实例）
> | Past-oriented（过去取向） | Present-oriented（现在取向） | Future-oriented（未来取向） |
> |---|---|---|
> | **China, Japan, UK, France; Belgium**<br>中国、日本、英国、法国；比利时 | **Philippines / Filipinos, Spain, Latin Americans, African Americans**<br>菲律宾／菲律宾人、西班牙、拉丁美洲人、非裔美国人 | **U.S. dominant culture**<br>美国主导文化 |

### 2.5 Activity Orientation（活动取向）

> **Activity orientation refers to how people of a culture view activity.**

**活动取向**指一个文化群体如何看待活动。

Three orientations are distinguished: **Doing, Being and Being-in-Becoming**.

三种取向分别是**做事取向、存在取向和成为取向**。

#### (1) Doing Orientation（做事取向）

> The doing-oriented culture stresses activity and action.

做事取向文化强调**活动和行动**。

**Associated culture（对应文化）：Dominant American / U.S. culture（美国主导文化）。**

The focus is on activity that can often be measured through external accomplishments. Planning, problem-solving, decisions, deadlines and schedules are important.

关注能够通过**外部成就衡量**的活动；重视计划、解决问题、决策、期限和日程安排。

**Example（例子）：** *What do you do? — “I design software.”*  
**例子：** “你做什么工作？”——“我设计软件。”（强调**做什么**。）

#### (2) Being Orientation（存在取向）

A being orientation refers to spontaneous expressions of human personality. It emphasizes accepting people, events and ideas as they occur, indulging existing desires and working for the moment.

存在取向强调**人的个性自然流露**，接纳自然发生的人、事和观念，重视当下的愿望与体验。

**Associated examples（相关文化实例）：Latino cultures, especially Mexican examples（拉丁美洲文化，尤其是墨西哥人的例子）。**

In Mexican examples, interpersonal relations may be valued more than accomplishments, and families and friends may talk at length. A comparison of Chinese and Western values also associates Chinese culture with **Being (who you are)**, emphasizing status, social roles and character.

在墨西哥人的相关例子中，**人际关系**可能比个人成就更受重视，亲友之间也倾向于长时间交谈。中西文化对照中，中国文化也被归为**存在取向（强调“你是谁”）**，重视身份、社会角色和品格。

**Example（例子）：** *What do you do? — “I am a manager.”*  
**例子：** “你做什么工作？”——“我是一名经理。”（强调**身份**。）

#### (3) Being-in-Becoming Orientation（成为取向）

> Being-in-Becoming culture stresses the idea of development and growth.

成为取向文化强调**发展与成长**。

**Associated traditions（对应传统）：Hinduism and Buddhism（印度教与佛教）。**

The goal is not an externally measurable achievement such as awards, status or money, but the internal development of the whole person. Eastern meditation provides an example.

其目标并非奖项、地位、金钱等外部可衡量的成就，而是**个人整体的内在发展**；某些东方冥想方式就是相关实例。

| Orientation | Main Emphasis | 中文翻译 |
|---|---|---|
| **Doing** | What you are doing; external accomplishments | **做事**：正在做什么、外在成就 |
| **Being** | Who you are; spontaneous experience and relationships | **存在**：你是谁、当下体验与人际关系 |
| **Being-in-Becoming** | Becoming and self-development | **成为**：成长与自我发展 |

### 2.6 Relational Orientation（人际关系取向）

Three orientations are **Hierarchy, Group and Individual**.

人际关系取向包括**等级取向、群体取向和个人取向**。

| Orientation | Cultural Example | English Explanation | 中文翻译 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hierarchy** | China（中国） | Obey authority; know your place; treat others according to their position; look to leading figures. | **等级取向**：服从权威、明确自己的位置、依身份对待他人、听取领导者意见。 |
| **Group** | Native Americans（美洲原住民） | Respond to what others think and feel; mutual dependence; loyalty; consult one's group. | **群体取向**：关注他人的想法和感受、相互依赖、忠诚、重视群体意见。 |
| **Individual** | United States（美国） | Express your own feelings and ideas; be independent and self-reliant; make your own decisions. | **个人取向**：表达自己的感受和观点、独立自主、自行决策。 |

**Hierarchy in Chinese Values（中国文化中的等级取向）**

Society consists of a series of hierarchies. A person at the top of one hierarchy may be at the bottom of another hierarchy above it. Position may be based on **age, family social position or academic achievement**.

社会由一系列等级关系构成。一个人在某一等级关系中可能位居高位，却在更大的等级体系中处于较低位置。地位可能由**年龄、家庭社会地位或学业成就**等因素决定。

**Example（例子）：** Caring for elderly people in the family（照顾家庭中的长辈）。

## 3. Limitations and Conclusion（模型局限与结论）

### 3.1 Limitations of Kluckhohn's Model（模型的局限）

> 1. Not everyone in a culture has the same basic values.
> 2. Cultures change over time.
> 3. The concept of basic values is itself a generalization.

1. 同一文化中的所有人并不一定具有相同的基本价值观。
2. 文化会随着时间发生变化。
3. “基本价值观”的概念本身就是一种概括。

### 3.2 Conclusion（结论）

> Within any culture, a preferred set of solutions will be chosen by most people. However, not all people from a culture will make exactly the same set of choices, and, in fact, some people from each culture will select other alternatives.

在任何文化中，大多数人都会倾向于选择某一组受到普遍认可的解决方式。但**并非同一文化中的每个人都会作出完全相同的选择**，有些人会选择其他方式。

For example, European American culture is described as generally emphasizing **doing**, the **future**, **control over nature**, and **individualism**, but not everyone identified with that culture shares all these beliefs.

例如，欧美裔美国文化总体上被描述为强调**行动、未来、控制自然和个人主义**，但并非每一个属于该文化群体的人都持有这些观念。
