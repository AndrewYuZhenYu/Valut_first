## 1. Edward T. Hall and Proxemics（爱德华·T·霍尔与空间关系学）

### 1.1 Edward T. Hall

**Edward T. Hall** was an American anthropologist, a pioneer author in intercultural communication, and a founding father of intercultural communication. He introduced the concept of **Proxemics**.

**爱德华·T·霍尔（Edward T. Hall）** 是美国人类学家、跨文化交际领域的先驱作家，也是跨文化交际学的奠基人之一。他提出了 **Proxemics（空间关系学／近体学）** 的概念。

His works include *The Hidden Dimension*, *The Silent Language*, and *Beyond Culture*.

其相关著作包括《隐秘的维度》《无声的语言》和《超越文化》。

### 1.2 Proxemics（空间关系学／近体学）

> [!definition] Proxemics
> **Proxemics is the study of human use of space and the effects that population density has on behaviour, communication, and social interaction.**
>
> **空间关系学**研究人类对空间的使用，以及人口密度对行为、交流和社会互动的影响。

Hall identified **four kinds of interpersonal distance**.

霍尔将人与人之间的空间距离划分为**四类**。

| Distance | Range | 中文及适用关系 |
|---|---:|---|
| **Public distance** | **> 360 cm** | **公众距离**：360 厘米以上 |
| **Social distance** | **120–360 cm** | **社交距离**：120—360 厘米 |
| **Personal distance** | **45–120 cm** | **个人距离**：45—120 厘米；伸手能够碰到对方，虽然认识却没有特别密切的关系 |
| **Intimate distance** | **0–45 cm** | **亲密距离**：0—45 厘米；通常为亲人、熟悉的朋友、情侣或夫妻 |

## 2. Context and Cultural Context（语境与文化语境）

### 2.1 What Is Context?（什么是语境？）

**high-context culture and low-context culture depending on the degree to which meaning comes from the settings or from the words being exchanged**

**高语境文化与低语境文化**的区别在于：交流中的意义在多大程度上来自**交流情境**，又在多大程度上来自**交流双方使用的语言**。

> [!important] Context（语境）
> Context can be understood through the amount of information that both sides already know and share when communicating.
>
> **语境就是双方聊天时，共同了解的信息量大小。**

**Example — Cultural Background（文化背景）**

If you tell an American a joke that depends on specifically Chinese cultural background and shared knowledge, the listener will not understand it. The problem is a lack of common cultural knowledge and therefore a difference in context.

举个简单例子：你给一个美国人讲一个中国段子，他必然是听不懂的。因为他没有文化背景，你们没有这部分的共同知识，术语一些来说，就是你们的语境不同。

### 2.2 High-context and Low-context Culture（高语境文化与低语境文化）

Edward T. Hall distinguished two kinds of cultural context: **high-context culture** and **low-context culture**. High-context cultures are described as often having a long and rich cultural heritage.

爱德华·霍尔将语境分为两种：**高语境**和**低语境**。高语境多数是那些文化底蕴很深的国家。

**High-context culture:** Meaning may be rich even when few words are used. Much can be understood without being fully stated; short expressions and sayings may carry considerable meaning.

**高语境文化：** 所谓高语境文化，乃是指内在意涵丰富而语词简约的，换成中国话来说，就是言简意赅，甚至只能意会而难以言传。

**In everyday life, famous sayings and aphorisms can be examples of high-context expressions. Some people adopt them as life mottos. “天行健君子以自强不息” is given as an example.**

**在日常生活中，一些名言警句，就属于高语境，许多人也将这种高语境作为座右铭，因为，这种语境实在是太高了，引人向往并作为人生所执，像“天行健君子以自强不息”，就是典型的高语境语种。**

**Low-context culture:** Communication is concrete, detailed, and explicit. Product instructions, addresses, and telephone numbers are examples of information typically conveyed with precise verbal details.

**低语境文化：** 而与高语境相反的就是低语境文化，低语境具有具体、详实的特点，同时显琐碎，唠唠叨叨，事无巨细，就是低语境了。在这里，产品说明书，或者转达一个朋友的住址或电话号码，就是低语境模式，它是社会日常交流和沟通的基本内容。

### 2.3 An Invitation to Coffee（邀请喝咖啡的案例）

**When a person from a low-context culture enters a high-context culture, the likelihood of being misunderstood increases. Consider an invitation to two friends to have coffee together at a popular bookstore café the next evening. Tina, from a low-context background, replies that she needs to study and thanks the inviter. Li, from a high-context background, replies that it sounds nice, but does not come.**

当低语境文化背景的人到了高语境文化中，他受到误解的可能性就大大增加了。为了说明这一点，我们假设这样的一个情境。你想邀请两位朋友明天晚上一同去一家十分受欢迎的书店咖啡厅喝咖啡。来自低语境文化背景的朋友蒂娜对你说“不了，我明天晚上要学习呢，但还是谢谢你的邀请”。而另一个来自于高语境文化背景的朋友李对你说“那很不错啊”，但是，之后你会很惊讶地发现他并没有如约去喝咖啡。

People raised in high-context cultures may find it difficult to say “no.” Even when refusing, they may use indirect language out of concern for offending others.

在高语境文化中成长的人，他们很难对别人说不。即便是他们真的拒绝某事，他们也会因为害怕得罪别人而婉转其辞。

### 2.4 The Context Scale（语境阶梯）

**No culture stays exclusively at either end of the context scale. Some cultures occupy relatively higher positions and others lower positions. American culture is described as being close to the lower end, while China is placed at the high-context extreme.**

> “尽管没有任何一种文化是专门停留在语境阶梯（context scale）的某一极的，然而，有的文化却处在较高的位置，其他一些又处在较低的地方。美国文化虽然不在底层，但是它确实靠近这一阶梯的底端。”
>
> “中国，这个拥有伟大而复杂文化的国家，却处在这个阶梯上高语境的极端。”

**原文背景：阿呆给领导送红包时，两个人的对话颇有意思。**

## 3. Definitions of High-context and Low-context Culture（高低语境文化的定义）

### 3.1 High-context Culture（高语境文化）

> [!definition] High-context Culture — Original Definition
> **A style of communication in which much of the information is contained in the contexts and nonverbal cues rather than expressed explicitly in words.**
>
> 一种交际方式，其中大量信息包含在语境与非语言线索中，而不是通过词语直接、明确地表达。

> **In a high-context message, meaning is in the situation and the background, roles, and relationships of the speakers.**
>
> 在高语境信息中，意义存在于交流情境以及说话者的背景、角色和相互关系之中。

### 3.2 Low-context Culture（低语境文化）

> [!definition] Low-context Culture — Original Definition
> **A style of communication in which much of the information is conveyed in words rather than in nonverbal cues and contexts.**
>
> 一种交际方式，其中大量信息通过词语传递，而不是主要依靠非语言线索和语境。

> **low- context communicators (most U.S. Americans) prefer to make information very explicit—expressed in words**
>
> 低语境交际者（此处以大多数美国人为例）倾向于把信息表达得十分明确，即使用语言直接说明。

### 3.3 Information in High-context Communication（高语境中的信息传递）

**In high-context messages, meaning is not necessarily contained in words. Information is provided through gestures, the use of space, and even silence.**

在高语境信息中，意义不一定存在于词语之中。信息还可以通过**手势、空间的使用，甚至沉默**来表达。

**Meaning is also conveyed through status (age, sex, education, family background, title, and affiliation).**

意义也通过**身份地位**来表达，包括年龄、性别、教育、家庭背景、头衔以及所属关系等。

Characteristics of high-context messages include:

高语境信息具有以下特点：

- **much information is implied** — 大量信息是隐含的。
- **includes the situation or surrounding circumstances,** — 包括情境或周围环境。
- **relationships of the communicators,** — 交际者之间的关系。
- **their family background,** — 他们的家庭背景。
- **title, age, sex, education, status,** — 头衔、年龄、性别、教育、地位。
- **social networks** — 社会关系网络。
- **little provided in the verbal message itself** — 言语信息本身提供的信息很少。

Communication in high-context cultures may be indirect and may convey information through facial expressions, gestures, eye contact, and cultural implications rooted in shared knowledge.

高语境国家说话更委婉，有时甚至用表情，动作，眼神来传递信息，因为大家相互之间存在着大量的共有文化，传递信息的方式从语言升级到了文化暗示。

### 3.4 Information in Low-context Communication（低语境中的信息传递）

Characteristics of low-context messages include:

低语境信息具有以下特点：

- **the majority of the information is contained in the verbal code** — 大部分信息包含在言语编码中。
- **the message is stated clearly and explicitly** — 信息表达清晰、明确。
- **without depending on the context of the communication** — 不依赖交际语境。

Low-context cultures are described as relying primarily on language because of population mobility and cultural diversity. Expressions of feeling may therefore be relatively direct and explicit.

低语境国家，由于民族多样性，以及人群的高流动性，导致在发展的过程中，积累的共同文化相对较少，传递信息的主要方式依旧来自语言。情感会更外露和直白（含蓄了对方可能会搞不明白）。

Examples of low-context countries include **the United States, Canada, Germany, Switzerland, Sweden, Norway, Finland, and Denmark**.

低语境（LowerContext Culture）国家有**美国、加拿大、德国、瑞士、瑞典、挪威、芬兰、丹麦**。

Such cultures emphasize detailed arrangements and precise schedules, with comparatively less emphasis on the surrounding context. Their behavioral system is described as drawing from Aristotelian logic and linear thinking.

这些国家的文化都是重视具体细节安排与精确的时间表，不注重环境的作用。他们的行为系统源于亚里斯多德的逻辑与线形思维。

### 3.5 A Cultural Context Continuum（文化语境连续体）

Cultures can be placed along a continuum from **higher context** to **lower context**, rather than being treated as only two isolated categories.

不同文化可以沿着从**高语境**到**低语境**的连续体排列，而不仅是两个彼此孤立的类别。

**Higher context → Lower context:**

Japanese → Chinese → Korean → African American → Native American → Arab → Greek → Latin → Italian → English → French → American → Scandinavian → German → German-Swiss.

**从高语境向低语境排列：**

日本、中国、韩国、非裔美国人、美国原住民、阿拉伯、希腊、拉丁、意大利、英国、法国、美国、斯堪的纳维亚、德国、德裔瑞士。

### 3.6 Verbal and Nonverbal Communication（言语与非言语交际）

Different cultures place different importance on verbal communication. In the United States, articulate people who express themselves fluently may be more attractive; in Korea, people who appear less talkative may be considered more trustworthy.

不同的文化对言语交际的重要性的认识是不一样的，在美国，往往口齿伶俐，善于口头表达的人更吸引人；而在韩国，更受信任的却是口头表达稍显木纳之人。

In low-context communication, nonverbal behavior may receive relatively little attention. In high-context communication, facial expressions, behavior, interaction speed, place, subtle gestures, feelings, and other environmental details can carry significant information.

在低语境文化里，人们往往对一些非语言交际行为视而不见；而在高语境文化里，交际者的面部表情、行动、交往速度、交往地点、难以言说的情绪、微妙的手势及其他周围环境细节等都是丰富的信息符号，给敏感的交际者以无限的信息与内涵。

## 4. Differences Between High-context and Low-context Cultures（高低语境文化的差异）

### 4.1 People and Events（人与事件）

**Low-context culture:** People are expected to separate individuals from events, even when doing so may affect personal relationships. In organizations, work relationships and criticism directed at the matter itself are emphasized.

**低语境文化：** 1、低语境文化要求交流者将人与事件分开来，尽管有时这样做会牺牲个人关系。在组织中，人们强调的是工作关系，而不是个人关系，批评也是对事不对人。

**High-context culture:** People may not separate a matter from the person involved. Criticism of a matter may be regarded as criticism of an individual. Saving face is important.

**高语境文化：** 相反，高语境文化往往不把事件与人分开，批评某件事就等于批评某个或某些人。在高语境文化中，人们往往有很强的保全面子的动机。

### 4.2 Direct and Indirect Communication（直接与间接交际）

**Low-context culture:** People tend to communicate directly, request detailed information, and guide the communication process. During interpersonal conflicts, they may present many facts and adopt a comparatively confrontational approach.

**低语境文化：** 2.低语境文化中的人使用非常直接的交流方式，他们总想获得大量信息并引导交流程序。例如在发生和解决人际冲突时，人们一般要摆出大量事实，具有一定的对抗性。

**High-context culture:** People tend to communicate more indirectly. Direct communication may cause loss of face or damage group harmony.

**高语境文化：** 相反，高语境文化中的人使用较为间接的交流方式，对他们来说，直接的交流方式可能会使人丢面子，会破坏团体的和谐。

### 4.3 Business Negotiation（商务谈判）

**Low-context culture:** Negotiators tend to use linear logical thinking and reasoning to bargain.

**低语境文化：** 3.在商业谈判时，低语境文化中的人趋向于线性逻辑思维，他们在讨价还价时善用推理分析。

**High-context culture:** Negotiators tend to use less explicit methods, including feelings and intuition.

**高语境文化：** 相反，高语境文化谈判时通常使用较为“模糊”的方式，善用包括感觉和直觉在内的交流方式。 东方社会很多都是“高语境”社会。

### 4.4 General Comparison（总体对比）

| High-context Culture（高语境文化） | Low-context Culture（低语境文化） |
|---|---|
| **Implicit and reserved**（内隐、含蓄） | **Explicit and clear**（外显、明了） |
| **Encoded / implied messages**（暗码信息） | **Explicitly coded messages**（明码信息） |
| **More nonverbal encoding**（较多的非言语编码） | **More verbal encoding**（较多的言语编码） |
| **Responses are seldom openly expressed**（反应很少外露） | **Responses are openly expressed**（反应外露） |
| **Clear in-group/out-group distinction**（圈内外有别） | **Flexible in-group/out-group boundaries**（圈内外灵活） |
| **Close interpersonal relationships**（人际关系紧密） | **Less close interpersonal relationships**（人际关系不密切） |
| **High commitment**（高承诺） | **Low commitment**（低承诺） |
| **Highly flexible time management**（时间处理高度灵活） | **Highly organized time management**（时间高度组织化） |

**High-context culture:** implicit and reserved; encoded information; more nonverbal encoding; responses seldom openly expressed; different treatment of in-groups and out-groups; close interpersonal relationships; high commitment; highly flexible handling of time.

高语境文化，内隐，含蓄；暗码信息；较多的非言语编码；反应很少外露；圈内外有别；人际关系紧密；高承诺；时间处理高度灵活。

**Low-context culture:** explicit and clear; openly coded information; more verbal encoding; responses openly expressed; flexible in-group/out-group boundaries; less close interpersonal relationships; low commitment; highly organized time.

低语境文化，外显，明了；明码信息；较多的言语编码；反应外露；圈内外灵活；人际关系不密切；低承诺；时间高度组织化。

In brief, high-context communication may appear reserved or secretive, while low-context communication may appear highly verbal and redundant.

简而言之，高语境文化的交际，有时显得秘而不宣、藏头露尾；低语境文化的交际，显得多言而冗余。

### 4.5 Cultural Contexts: Information Sources（信息来源与语境）

| Lower Context（较低语境） | Higher Context（较高语境） |
|---|---|
| **Information provided explicitly, by words.** — 信息通过语言明确传递。 | **Information drawn from surroundings.** — 信息从周围情境中获取。 |
| **Nonverbal cues are ignored.** — 非语言线索被忽略。 | **Nonverbal cues are important.** — 非语言线索重要。 |
| **Need detailed background information.** — 需要详细的背景信息。 | **Free flow of information.** — 信息流动自由。 |
| **Consider knowledge a commodity.** — 将知识看作一种商品。 | **Maintain strong information networks.** — 维持强大的信息网络。 |

## 5. Characteristics of High-context and Low-context Culture（高低语境文化的特点）

### 5.1 Face Concern（面子关注）

> [!important] High-context Culture
> **In high-context cultures, people are extremely polite, trying to avoid threatening the “face” of their conversational partner.**
>
> 在高语境文化中，人们非常讲究礼貌，尽力避免威胁交谈对象的“面子”。

> [!important] Low-context Culture
> **In low-context cultures, people are more individualistic and more concerned about their own face rather than that of others. They mean what they say.**
>
> 在低语境文化中，人们更加重视个人主义，更关注自己的面子而非他人的面子；他们往往直接表达自身意思。

**When confronted with people from high-context cultures:**

- **People feel uncomfortable with the vagueness and ambiguity of the message** — 人们对信息的模糊性和歧义感到不适。
- **Become puzzled, frustrated, impatient or even irritated.** — 感到困惑、沮丧、不耐烦，甚至恼怒。

### 5.2 Case Analysis: Chinese Dean and Foreigner Teacher Jim（中国院长与外籍教师 Jim）

**Case Analysis — HC/LC**

- **Chinese Dean** — 中国院长
- **Foreigner Teacher Jim** — 外籍教师 Jim

**Example — “We will consider it.”**

> **In China, the words : “ we will consider it”.**
>
> **While Chinese usually take this sentence as a tentative decline or a half-decline at least, westerners might miss the implied message and experience disappointment when hearing the same answer again and again.**

**例子——“我们会考虑的。”**

在中国，“我们会考虑的”通常可能被理解为一种试探性的拒绝，或至少是半拒绝；西方交际者可能没有理解其中隐含的意思，反复听到相同答复后会感到失望。

- **In HC Culture — Quite Concern others' faces**  
  **在高语境文化中：** 非常关心他人的面子。
- **In LC Culture — Heavily rely on What people say**  
  **在低语境文化中：** 高度依赖人们说出的语言。

### 5.3 In-groups and Out-groups（内群体与外群体）

**In high-context cultures:**

**在高语境文化中：**

- **Strong, deep commitment between people.** — 人与人之间具有牢固而深厚的承诺。
- **Long-lasting and unchanging loyalties to families and friends.** — 对家人和朋友保持长期且稳定的忠诚。
- **A clear distinction between in-group members (intimate friends generally) and out-group members (casual acquaintances generally).** — 内群体成员（通常为亲密朋友）与外群体成员（通常为一般熟人）的界限较为明确。

**In low-context cultures:**

**在低语境文化中：**

- **The bonds between people are quite fragile.** — 人与人之间的联系相对较脆弱。
- **Group membership changes rapidly.** — 群体成员身份变化较快。

### 5.4 Friendship and Social Relationships（友谊与社会关系）

**In handling friendships, Chinese people are described as valuing old friendships and expecting great loyalty and mutual help. Longer-lasting friendships are treated as a sign of deeper bonds.**

在处理朋友关系方面，我们与西方国家的人也大不相同。我们重视老朋友的关系，对于朋友的期望值很高，即使不是桃源结义，也应情同手足。若是交流，最好是无所不谈；若有困难，应该倾囊相助。友谊保持的时间越久，越能显示出友情之深。

**In American society, frequent moves are said to make long-lasting friendships difficult to maintain. Friendship circles often change, and close friendships involving numerous duties and obligations may be avoided.**

但是，在美国这样的国家，人们一生之中往往需要迁移多次，要保持长久的友谊关系是十分困难的。因此，朋友的圈子常常变动。另一方面，美国人一般不愿意朋友关系过深带有许多责任和义务。

**Edward Stewart & Milton Bennett are quoted as discussing this distinction:**

**引用：**

> “虽然美国人保持许多友好的非正式的关系，但是他们极少有那种很深而又维系多年的友谊关系，在这种关系中朋友之间变得相互依赖。理想的境界是美国式的友谊建立在自然发生、相互之间的吸引以及温馨的个人感情的基础之上。人们选择自己的朋友，同时，他们又把朋友关系与社会或工作义务分开。”

## 6. Consequences for Intercultural Communication（对跨文化交际的影响）

### 6.1 Avoiding Misunderstandings（避免误解）

People can display both high-context and low-context communication preferences. Paying attention to how the other person communicates can help prevent misunderstandings and conflicts.

其实我们生活的周围，也大致可以分为两类人，一类是偏高语境者，另一类是偏低语境者。在与不同类型的人沟通的时候，一定要更注意言行，防止对方产生误解，双方出现矛盾。

### 6.2 How High-context and Low-context Affect Intercultural Communication（高低语境如何影响跨文化交际）

> **Lack of enough data, people from low-context cultures often feel uncomfortable with the vagueness and ambiguity and will ask very blunt, inappropriate questions, which will make high-context culture members become impatient and irritated.**
>
> 由于缺乏足够的信息，低语境文化背景的人往往难以接受表达中的模糊和不明确，可能提出过于直接或不恰当的问题，使高语境文化成员变得不耐烦、恼怒。

> **People in high-context cultures perceive low-context people less credible.**
>
> 高语境文化中的人可能认为低语境文化中的人不那么值得信赖。

> **Conflict are differently perceived and responded to.**
>
> 不同语境文化中的人对冲突的理解和回应方式存在差异。

### 6.3 How to Avoid Problems（如何避免交际问题）

- **High-context: discreetly and subtly.**  
  **高语境：** 谨慎、委婉地表达。
- **Low-context: directly spell it out.**  
  **低语境：** 直接把意思讲清楚。

### 6.4 Which Is Better: High-context or Low-context?（高语境还是低语境更好？）

Neither can be judged as universally better. High-context communication may be more efficient in a shared cultural setting, while low-context communication may help people complete tasks and procedures more precisely by reducing ambiguity.

这个很难笼统的说谁优谁劣。从文化角度来看，高语境更具有沟通效率，但从执行事务的执行角度来看，似乎低语境更容易把每个环节做的更好，从事人员的方向不会被被类似高语境的暗示所迷惑，而误入歧途。

## 7. High-context vs. Low-context: Further Comparisons（高低语境的进一步比较）

### 7.1 Communication, Knowledge, and Decisions（沟通、知识与决策）

| High-context | Low-context |
|---|---|
| **Less verbally explicit communication; less written/formal information.** — 较少明确的语言表达及书面／正式信息。 | **Rule oriented; people play by external rules.** — 以规则为导向，遵循外部规则。 |
| **More internalized understandings of what is communicated.** — 对所传递信息有更多内化的共同理解。 | **More knowledge is codified, public, external, and accessible.** — 更多知识被编码、公开、外化并易于获取。 |
| **Multiple cross-cutting ties and intersections with others.** — 与他人存在多重交叉联系。 | **Sequencing; separation of time, space, activities, and relationships.** — 将时间、空间、活动和关系按顺序进行区分。 |
| **Long-term relationships.** — 长期关系。 | **More interpersonal connections of shorter duration.** — 更多持续时间较短的人际联系。 |
| **Strong boundaries: who belongs and who is an outsider.** — 明确区分内部成员与外部人员。 | **Knowledge is more often transferable.** — 知识更容易转移和传播。 |
| **Knowledge is situational, relational.** — 知识具有情境性和关系性。 | **Task-centered.** — 以任务为中心。 |
| **Decisions and activities focus around personal** — 决策和活动围绕个人……（此句至此，保持原有未完句形式。） | **Decisions and activities focus around what needs to be done and the division of responsibilities.** — 决策和活动围绕任务完成和责任分工进行。 |

### 7.2 Relationships, Identity, and Authority（关系、身份与权威）

| High context（高语境） | Low context（低语境） |
|---|---|
| **Relationships depend on trust, build up slowly, are stable. One distinguishes between people inside and people outside one's circle.** | **Relationships begin and end quickly. Many people can be inside one's circle; circle's boundary is not clear.** |
| 关系依赖信任，建立较慢、相对稳定；区分圈内和圈外的人。 | 关系的建立与结束较快；圈内可能包含较多人，圈子的界限并不明确。 |
| **How things get done depends on relationships with people and attention to group process.** | **Things get done by following procedures and paying attention to the goal.** |
| 事情如何完成取决于人际关系以及对群体过程的关注。 | 通过遵守程序和关注目标来完成事情。 |
| **One's identity is rooted in groups (family, culture, work).** | **One's identity is rooted in oneself and one's accomplishments.** |
| 个体身份根植于所属群体，如家庭、文化和工作群体。 | 个体身份根植于自身及个人成就。 |
| **Social structure and authority are centralized; responsibility is at the top. Person at top works for the good of the group.** | **Social structure is decentralized; responsibility goes further down (is not concentrated at the top).** |
| 社会结构和权力较为集中，责任在上层；领导者为群体整体利益工作。 | 社会结构较为分散，责任向较低层级分配，而不集中于最高层。 |

## 8. Case Studies（案例研究）

### 8.1 Case Study 1 — Malaysian and European American（马来西亚人与欧洲裔美国人）

**Original dialogue（英文对话原文）**

> **Malaysian:** Can I ask you a question?  
> **European American:** Yes, of course.  
> **M:** Do you know what time it is?  
> **E:** Yes, it’s 2 o’clock.  
> **M:** Might you have a little soup left in the pot?  
> **E:** What? I don’t understand.  
> **M:** I will be on campus teaching until 9 o’clock tonight, a very long day for any person, let alone a hungry one!  
> **E:** Would you like me to drive you to a restaurant off campus so you can have lunch?  
> **M:** What a good idea you have!

**中文翻译**

> **马来西亚人：** 我能问你一个问题吗？  
> **欧洲裔美国人：** 当然可以。  
> **马来西亚人：** 你知道现在几点了吗？  
> **欧洲裔美国人：** 知道，现在两点。  
> **马来西亚人：** 锅里可能还剩下一点汤吗？  
> **欧洲裔美国人：** 什么？我不明白。  
> **马来西亚人：** 我今晚要在学校一直教课到九点。对任何人来说这都是漫长的一天，更别说一个饿着肚子的人了！  
> **欧洲裔美国人：** 你想让我开车带你去校外的餐馆吃午饭吗？  
> **马来西亚人：** 你这个主意真不错！

The Malaysian's indirect questions and comments communicate a need that the European American does not immediately infer from the spoken words.

马来西亚人通过间接提问与陈述传达自己的需求，而欧洲裔美国人没有立即从字面表达中推断出其意图。

### 8.2 Case Study 2 — Asking for Leave（请假案例）

**Situation:** In Hong Kong, a Chinese policeman (A) goes to his British superior (B) and asks for leave to take his mother to hospital.

**情境：** 在香港，一位中国警察（A）前去向英国上级（B）请假，以便带母亲去医院。

**Original dialogue（英文对话原文）**

> **A:** Sir?  
> **B:** Yes, what is it?  
> **A:** My mother is not very well, sir.  
> **B:** So?  
> **A:** She has to go into hospital, sir.  
> **B:** Well, get on with it. What do you want?  
> **A:** On Thursday, sir.  
> **B:** Bloody hell, man. What do you want?  
> **A:** Nothing, sir.

**中文翻译**

> **A：** 长官？  
> **B：** 什么事？  
> **A：** 我母亲身体不太好，长官。  
> **B：** 所以呢？  
> **A：** 她得住院，长官。  
> **B：** 那就直说吧。你到底想要什么？  
> **A：** 星期四，长官。  
> **B：** 见鬼，你到底想要什么？  
> **A：** 没什么，长官。

### 8.3 Study of the Case（案例分析）

> **High context ↔ low context**
>
> **From the case, we see that meanings do not come from the verbal language alone.**
>
> 从这一案例可见，意义并不只来自言语本身。
>
> **They are also implied from the context in which communication takes place.**
>
> 意义还可能隐含在交际发生时的具体语境中。
>
> **In other words, setting and environment help determine the words and actions we generate and the meanings we give to the words and actions of other people.**
>
> 换言之，交际情境和环境会影响我们使用的语言与行为，也影响我们对他人语言与行为所赋予的意义。

### 10.3 Exercise 3 — Understanding Refusal（练习三：理解拒绝）

**Question 1: Did my daughter really mean it when she refused her classmate’s offer?**

**问题一：当我女儿拒绝同学的提议时，她真的想拒绝吗？**

> **No, she didn't really mean it.**
>
> 不，她并不是真的想拒绝。
>
> **She wanted to have one hamburger very much. She refused her classmate's offer just because she was Chinese. She knew the hamburger was quite expensive, so she didn't want to ask a favor of her classmate. If she had done so, she would have thought she had taken financial advantage of her classmate.**
>
> 她非常想吃一个汉堡包，但由于自身的中国文化背景而拒绝了同学的好意。她知道汉堡包价格不便宜，因此不想让同学为自己破费；若接受，她会觉得自己在经济上占了同学的便宜。

**Question: What can we learn about her culture? Use Hall’s culture context model to illustrate it.**

**问题：从中可以了解她的文化具有怎样的特点？请用霍尔文化语境模型说明。**

> **Chinese culture is a typical high-context culture in which people's mode of communication is rather indirect and tentative.**
>
> 中国文化是典型的高语境文化，人们的交际方式较为间接和试探性。
>
> **Sometimes they don't mean what they say simply for the sake of courtesy.**
>
> 有时为了礼貌，人们说出口的话并不代表其真正意图。

**Tentative:** hesitant; exploratory; provisional.  
**Tentative：** 犹豫不决的；试探性的；暂定的。

### 10.4 Exercise 3 — Understanding Invitations（练习三：理解邀请）

**Question 2: What makes the Philippine girl consider the invitation as genuine?**

**问题二：为什么菲律宾女生认为这一邀请是真诚的？**

> **Generally, Westerners consider an invitation as genuine if it specifies a definite time, place and activity. In this case, all three criteria were very clear, so the Philippine girl took the invitation as genuine, and was very happy to accept it.**
>
> 通常情况下，如果邀请明确说明了时间、地点和活动内容，西方人便会认为这是真诚的邀请。该案例中的三个条件都很明确，因此菲律宾女生将邀请视为真诚的邀请，并且非常高兴地接受。

**Question: What can we learn about her culture? Use Hall’s culture context model to illustrate it.**

**问题：我们可以从她的文化中了解到什么？请用霍尔文化语境模型说明。**

> **What she didn't know was that this invitation was not intended to be genuine by the girl who invited her, her Chinese classmate, who extended the offer just out of courtesy.**
>
> 她不知道的是，提出邀请的中国同学并不是真的打算邀请她，而只是出于礼貌才这样说。
>
> **For people raised in low-context cultures, who adopt the directed frank mode of communication, they must take care to discern the real intentions of those raised in high-context cultures.**
>
> 在低语境文化中成长、习惯于直接坦率交际方式的人，需要注意辨别高语境文化背景的交际者真正想表达的意图。

**Discern:** to recognize or distinguish.  
**Discern：** 识别；辨别。
